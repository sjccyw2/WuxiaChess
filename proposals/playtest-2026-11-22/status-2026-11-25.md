# Playtest review: status on 2026-11-25

The producer asked, after 23 commits of his own (ROADMAP 0.36 to 0.42 and the prose edit of 2026-11-24), whether the review's comments still hold. Every finding of `README.md` beside this page, and every row of `../text-declutter-2026-11-23.md` not built in 0.35, was checked again against the game at commit `3675176`, in a headless browser at the formats each concerns. Findings already marked fixed were checked again for regressions. Nothing in the game was changed.

Three subagents did the checking, each reproducing a finding in the browser where it is about something a player sees and reading the code where it is about code. The lead confirmed in the code the two claims with most weight: the out-of-reach tap after a walk (below, R-14) and 返回 on the hero pick after a lost run (new problem 1).

**Verdicts:** **Fixed** (gone), **Partly** (some of it remains: the remainder is what is listed), **Still valid** (as described).

**Fixed:** R-10 (no guided first battle: the tutorial, 0.32), R-15 (only a figure's feet were its target: 0.33), R-27 (a healing art with no prompt).

**The ten that matter most now**, in order:

1. **R-14 is back in a new form.** Since 0.37 no art is shown as ready after a walk, and the explanation added in 0.33 only runs while an art is ready (`unitClick` in `game/src/app.js` tests `!aimHidden`). So after a walk, the normal state, a tap on an enemy out of reach, a diagonal neighbour included, opens 敵情查探 at once with no reason: the playtest's commonest stumble. The fix is to give the same first-tap message whenever the selected hero could strike, art shown or not.
2. **R-02 and R-03: progress can still be lost silently.** Two tabs overwrite each other's save, and the wiki's 進入遊戲 opens the second tab; 另開新局 replaces a run with no question.
3. **R-04: a scroll swipe on an equipment card equips it** on a phone, with no message.
4. **R-44 and R-45: on the last board 求敗 looks like every master**, and nothing warns that they all go for the leader (Q-N129).
5. **R-53: the wiki's front page gives away the ending** (Q-N127). One tap from the title.
6. **R-29: two missing files on boards with an object.** Cause found: `ActorLayer.advance()` in `game/src/actors/actors.js` asks for the sheet of every piece with an action track, and a struck object (the qin, the drum, the posts) has no `mini`. A one-line guard.
7. **R-59 and R-60: on the keyboard Enter cancels** the end of turn and the attack preview, and Space skips the film and the whole prologue.
8. **R-51 and R-52: text under 12 px and targets under 44 px** remain on most pages outside the battle screen, which now passes. Itemised below.
9. **R-17 to R-20: the battle screen on phones and desktop.** One tap still walks the preselected leader; the default view is small on phones; on desktop the arts list covers the board and pieces tell nothing under the pointer.
10. **R-36 to R-38: 整備.** A learned art is not equipped; on a phone the learn button and the leader's pane sit under the footer.

**New problems found while checking** (section 4) include one bug: after a lost run, 返回 on the new run's hero pick shows the old lost board.

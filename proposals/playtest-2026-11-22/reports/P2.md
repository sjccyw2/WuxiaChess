# Playtest report P2: 志豪, phone portrait (390 × 844, touch only)

## 1. Who I was and what I covered

**Persona:** 志豪, 27, a software tester in Kaohsiung and a tactics-game veteran (Fire Emblem, 曹操傳, 天地劫, Into the Breach). He knows 金庸 only from TV, taps through dialogue fast, and wants numbers, builds and modern conveniences.

**Format:** phone portrait, 390 × 844, touch only. Browser on port 9302.

**What I played:**
- A new run from the title screen: opening film skipped, story pages fast-tapped and skipped, hero pick (抓子) with a swap and a leader change.
- **Board 1, 青竹渡:** 軍情, 整備 (all three tabs, learned and equipped a new art for each of the three heroes, swapped weapons and armour), placement, then **three full honest rounds**. I used select, move, 撤步, the art panel, the forecast, enemy info, the threat overlay, the grid toggle, the camera panel (rotate, top-down, zoom, 定位俠客, 地形圖), 隊伍, 目標/軍情, 藥囊, 選單, 設定 and end turn. I finished with 速勝・支線 in round 4.
- **After the win:** story (fast-tapped), the shop 通吃坊 (record, shelves, the dice game 三盅通吃, buying a weapon manual and a pill, the team page inside the shop). I reloaded the page mid-shop. In 整備 I reordered by tap and by drag, moved the leader by dragging, read the hero pane, granted a manual (授業武譜), learned an art from it and slotted it, and read 合招譜.
- **Board 2, 紅楓驛:** about 2.5 honest rounds using 亢龍有悔, 五行布陣 and the new 彈指神通 stun (the forecast showed it working), then 速勝・支線.
- **Board 3, 蘆花汀:** three honest rounds (poison needle, 石壁劍招), then 速勝・支線. I reloaded mid-整備 (from the shop's 整理隊伍) after learning an art.
- **試局 空山琴臺 (guard the 七弦琴):** three rounds. I watched reinforcements arrive, then **lost on purpose with 速敗** in round 3 and followed the loss flow to the end screen (眼破).
- **試局 無刻漏臺 (break two 星索樁):** three rounds, then 速勝.

**Not reached:** boards 4 to 10 of a real run, the true final board, and a real (non-dev) defeat by losing the leader.

**Rig trouble:**
- `goto` and `reload` timed out twice (30 s) but the page had loaded each time.
- The shared `c.sh` helper in the scratchpad pointed at another tester's port. I noticed at once and used my own copy. No browser restart was needed.

## 2. How it felt: play diary (志豪's voice)

1. **Title screen.** I tap 入夢 expecting the game. Instead a 操作指南 with 15 collapsed sections opens. I close it at once; I will learn by playing. Mildly annoyed. *(004)*
2. **Opening film.** A 略過 button is visible at the bottom right from the first frame. One tap, no confirm, gone. That is exactly what I want. Pleased.
3. **Prologue dialogue.** I hammer 下一句 four times. It lands me on the hero pick and nothing breaks. OK.
4. **抓子 (hero pick).** Five names, keep three, pick a leader. Where are the stats? I get the name and the leader aura of the current leader only, and no HP/ATK/move or role. I picked 蕭峰 as leader because his aura had a number in it. That is guessing, not building. Frustrated.
5. **軍情 briefing.** The minimap with numbered start tiles, 局主/副手 markers, and win/lose/side objective written in plain numbers ("第十二回合結束前") is genuinely good. Happy.
6. **整備 → 武學.** A real skill tree with tiers and lines, and every node lists 1 武學點, 內力 11, 距離 1 格, 威力 36, 擊退 1. This is my favourite screen in the game. But after 修習 I must tap 前往招式配置, then pick an empty slot, then tap 配置此格. Four taps for "learn and use", times three heroes. Grumpy but engaged.
7. **裝備物資.** I swiped up on the item row to scroll, and that swipe *equipped* 雁翎刀 on 黃蓉, replacing her 青竹棒. It happened again on 小龍女's page. I did not mean to do either. Annoyed: on a phone a swipe is a scroll. *(027, 028)*
8. **Placement.** A clear hint banner, and tap-to-swap works. Fine.
9. **Round 1, moving.** The board is a rotated 3D view. I tapped a green tile and nothing happened. I tapped the unit and nothing happened. Then I tapped my own selected hero, and the game took it as "stay here" and opened the art panel with 撤步. It took a grid overlay and three tries to make one move. Irritated.
10. **The damage forecast (交鋒預覽).** Before/after HP bars, −42, enemy counter −6, 內力 cost, and terrain or art synergy (撞關 +7). This is Fire Emblem-grade information. Delighted.
11. **撤步 (undo).** It exists and works until you attack. Relief.
12. **The enemy threat toggle (⚠).** One tap paints every enemy's reach in purple. That is what I wanted from Into the Breach. Good. The camera panel (rotate, top-down, zoom %, 定位俠客, 地形圖 with terrain rules in numbers) is excellent too.
13. **Ending the turn.** The game asks "尚有 2 位俠客可行動" every single turn. Then I watch the whole enemy phase with no speed-up and no skip. There is nothing in 設定 for it either. Bored, and checking my phone.
14. **Post-battle story.** I fast-tapped 下一句. The box changes height every line, so the button moves. One tap landed outside the box, and I was thrown back to the **title screen** with a "繼續戰後劇情" button. Confused: did I lose progress? (No.) It happened twice. *(082, 084)*
15. **Shop 通吃坊.** It is one long scroll; the price tag doubles as the buy button; item stats are hidden behind ▶ toggles. The dice game 三盅通吃 is cute: a free pick of three, cups shuffle, and I got a 玄鐵護腕. Buying 《彈指神通》 was one tap with no confirm. Fine, quick.
16. **Granting the manual.** After I gave 彈指神通 to 小龍女 it said **先修前式: 淬鋒一重**, a requirement the shop listing never mentioned, and it says 每冊限授一人. I had to sink a point into 淬鋒 to use my purchase. A min-maxer feels cheated by hidden prerequisites.
17. **Board 2.** The stun forecast read "失神 · 田歸農失去下一次行動", and the boss was down 26 HP. That felt smart. But I tapped an enemy from out of range and simply got its info card, with no "out of range" hint. Meh.
18. **Five heroes.** The fifth hero's chip in 武學/裝備 sits off-screen at the right edge with no hint you can scroll. I almost missed that 令狐冲 had points to spend.
19. **空山琴臺 guard board.** The briefing explains it in one sentence ("守住七弦琴，撐到第八回合結束；或在此之前擊敗左冷禪"). The HUD shows "守 · 尚須 8 回合" counting down, and tapping the 琴 shows its HP and rules. Reinforcements are marked 援 on their spawn tiles a round ahead, with "敵援 · 下回合 2 名" in the HUD. The clearest special rule I have seen in a tactics game. Impressed.
20. **Losing (速敗).** I got poetic lines, a shop that is still open ("此處購得的物品與武譜將寄存，於下次啟程時領取"), then the 眼破 screen with 此輪破局 3/10, 累積的悟 3, 最遠走到 3 局. I understand the run is over. I do not understand what 悟 is for, or exactly what I keep. Shrug.
21. **無刻漏臺.** I like the post rules: 209 HP each, the boss takes −35% damage per standing post, and the info card says it. But the HUD has no "破 0/2" counter (the guard board had one). On the right side the 破 badge sits on top of the boss's 主 badge and HP bar. My leader got mauled from 188 to 100 in one enemy phase because I misread the reach. My fault, but the board is busy.
22. **Overall mood.** The depth is real and the numbers are there. But my thumbs spend more time fighting the 3D board and the multi-tap menus than thinking.

## 3. Findings

Screenshots are in scratch/p2/

### P2-01: A tap outside the story box exits to the title screen; the box resizes, so fast tapping misses 下一句

- **Severity:** Major
- **Type:** Flow / Touch
- **Where:** Story dialogue (戰後 and 臨陣 boxes)
- **What happened:** The dialogue box changes height with each line, so 下一句 moves. Its centre y was 525, 544, 510, 568 and 577 on different lines. A fast tap that lands just outside the box closes the story and drops to the title screen showing "繼續戰後劇情". Progress was kept, but it looks like a crash or exit.
- **What I expected:** Tapping anywhere advances the line (genre standard), or 下一句 stays fixed at the bottom of the screen, or a tap outside does nothing.
- **Steps to reproduce:**
  1. Win a board.
  2. In the 戰後 story, tap below the box, around (330, 600).
- **Evidence:**
  - scratch/p2/082-after-story.png
  - scratch/p2/084-outside-tap.png
- **Confidence:** Reproduced (2×)
- **Suggestion:** Make the backdrop tap advance the dialogue, and anchor the button row at a fixed spot.

### P2-02: Swiping to scroll on equipment cards equips the item

- **Severity:** Major
- **Type:** Touch / Bug
- **Where:** 整備 → 裝備物資, the 行囊 item cards (with ⠿ handle)
- **What happened:** A vertical swipe that started on 雁翎刀 equipped it on the shown hero, replacing her weapon (黃蓉: 青竹棒 → 雁翎刀; later 小龍女). There was no confirm or undo toast.
- **What I expected:** A swipe scrolls. Drag-to-equip only from the ⠿ handle, or after a long-press.
- **Steps to reproduce:**
  1. Open 整備 → 裝備物資.
  2. Drag from (124, 640) to (124, 300).
- **Evidence:**
  - scratch/p2/027-drag-equip2.png
  - scratch/p2/028-xln-equip.png
- **Confidence:** Reproduced (2×)
- **Suggestion:** Require a long-press before a drag starts on touch screens.

### P2-03: Picking tiles and targets on the 3D board is unreliable at phone size; out-of-range taps give no feedback

- **Severity:** Major
- **Type:** Touch / Feedback
- **Where:** Every battle, default oblique camera
- **What happened:**
  - On 青竹渡 R1, taps on green-highlighted tiles at (212, 398) and (205, 395) did nothing, twice each. It took a grid overlay and several tries to move.
  - Tapping the selected hero commits a "stay in place" move (see P2-10).
  - With an art armed, tapping an enemy outside its range opens 敵情查探 instead of saying "out of range". Seen on 空山琴臺 (費彬 was diagonal, not adjacent) and on 無刻漏臺 (a post behind an enemy).
  - Diagonal versus orthogonal adjacency is hard to read on the rotated grid. On 紅楓驛, zoomed out, tiles are about 19 px.
- **What I expected:** Tiles of 44 px or more, or a confirm step. An "超出射程" message when targeting out of range.
- **Steps to reproduce:**
  1. On 空山琴臺 R2, move 令狐冲 next to the stairs and arm 華山劍法.
  2. Tap 費彬.
- **Evidence:**
  - scratch/p2/035-r1-moved.png
  - scratch/p2/040-r1-try4.png
  - scratch/p2/144-guard-try.png
  - scratch/p2/147-guard-r2b.png
  - scratch/p2/155-posts-range.png
- **Confidence:** Reproduced. I confirmed with grid shots, so these were not mis-taps.
- **Suggestion:** When an art is armed, an out-of-range enemy tap should flash "超出射程". Consider a top-down default on phones.

# Playtest report: L3

## 1. Who I was and what I covered

- **Persona:** 阿哲, 40, a logistics worker and father of two in Hong Kong. He played 金庸群俠傳 on DOS as a boy and knows the novels. He plays in short bursts and is interrupted all the time.
- **Format:** phone held sideways, 844 × 390, touch only. I also checked 667 × 375, 568 × 320, 932 × 430 and 1024 × 768.
- **What I played:**
  - **Opening, no developer mode:** the opening film, the four-page prologue, the hero pick (抓子), the briefing (軍情) and preparation (整備).
  - **Board 1 (青竹渡), played honestly:** rounds 1 to 5, with interruptions at every kind of moment.
  - **Developer mode** (from here on):
    - finished board 1 with 速勝;
    - post-battle story, reward and shop (通吃坊), including the cup game;
    - 開五重境界, the difficulty page (選境界), then 跳至終局.
  - **Last board (谷底棋臺):**
    - played honestly for two rounds and lost for real (無名 was swarmed);
    - a second attempt: two presses of 局主半血, one round, a `reload`, then 速勝;
    - the ending film, the pages after it, the title screen afterwards and a new run.
  - **Other screens:** the loss pages, the guide, settings, and the wiki (front page, 群俠列傳, a hero page, 俠客對照, search, the 青竹渡 map).
  - **Back and tabs:** the `back` button and a second game tab.
  - **Other screen sizes:** title, 整備, battle and shop at the four extra sizes.
- **Not reached:**
  - I did not play four honest rounds on the last board. My honest attempt ended in a loss after round 2, and the second attempt had only one honest round before 速勝.
  - I did not play boards 2 to 9.
- **Rig trouble:**
  - The browser driver crashed once, straight after the `eval "window.open(...)"` step. I restarted it as the brief describes. The save survived, but screenshot numbering restarted at 001.   - The shell's output was cut short twice when chained commands ran through `taptext 略過`, but the screen always showed the expected state.

## 2. How it felt: a play diary

1. **Title, first time.** It is a clean screen with one big 入夢 button. "OK, a wuxia tactics game, let's go." *Curious.*
2. **Opening film.** Beautiful ink-comic panels, and 略過 sits where my thumb is. I locked the phone and the film carried on playing behind the lock screen, so I missed part of it. *Slightly cheated.*
3. **Turning the phone upright mid-film.** It started over from the beginning, and it did so again when I turned back. I had turned the phone twice to answer a message, and I watched the first 20 seconds three times. *Annoyed.*
4. **Reload in the prologue and in the hero pick.** The title offered only 入夢 again, so it looked as if I had lost everything. I had to sit through, or skip, the film and the prologue again. It did keep my three picks and my leader, which was a relief once I got there. *Confused, then relieved.*
5. **Reload once board 1 had started.** Now the title said 繼續第一局 with 另開新局 beside it. That is a clear "continue". *Reassured.*
6. **另開新局 by mistake.** There was no "are you sure?" at all. After two skips and one 落子 my previous run was gone. With a child pulling at my arm, that is a real risk. *Alarmed.*
7. **整備 after a reload.** My learned skill was kept, but I was back on the 陣容 tab with the wrong hero selected. A small thing. *Mild.*
8. **Battle reloads.** These were very good:
   - a moved-but-not-attacked hero was still moved, with 撤步 still offered;
   - my selected hero was still selected;
   - a reload during the enemy turn picked up the enemy phase;
   - the menu even says 進度已自動保存, and 戰場紀錄 kept the history.

   The camera did go back to the default wide view each time, and I had to pinch in again. *Trusting.*
9. **Reading the board at 390 px tall.** At the default zoom the tiles are tiny. Tapping an enemy that is out of reach opens a big 敵情查探 panel instead of telling me "too far". I pinched and dragged a lot. *Squinting.*
10. **The `back` button.** One press dumped me out of the game to a blank page. Old habit from browsing. *Startled*, though the save was fine.
11. **Second tab.** This was the worst moment of the day:
    - I opened the game in a second tab and won board 1 there, bought an item and played the cup game.
    - I went back to the old tab. It still showed the battle in round 5, with no warning, and I tapped 結束回合.
    - The victory, the gold and the item were all gone. The title went back to 繼續第一局.

    *Betrayed.*
12. **Post-battle story.** Lock, turn the phone, reload: every time it came back on exactly line 3／9, and the title said 繼續戰後劇情. *Delighted.* This is what I want everywhere.
13. **The shop and the cup game.** Charming: 韋小寶 is in character. The cup game also survived a reload (返回盅局). At 390 px tall, though, the cups and the prize name sit below the fold. *Pleased.*
14. **Last board briefing.** The goal is clear in words: bring 求敗 down to 1 氣血, and the others need not be beaten. The map legend, though, marks all six red squares as 局主, and on the board every enemy carries a 主 tag. I could not tell which one was 求敗 without tapping each. *Puzzled.*
15. **Playing the last board.** Things went wrong fast:
    - a tap near 無名 moved him one square by accident (撤步 saved me);
    - the 服用一份 button for potions was hidden below the fold inside the pack;
    - after the 局主半血 presses about twelve 主 tags piled into one illegible clump.

    I lost in round 2. *Overwhelmed.*
16. **The loss page.**
    - 還差著一點 reads well.
    - It shows "此輪破局 10 / 10" next to "最遠走到 1 局", which is odd (probably from the developer jump).
    - It says the ten boards must all be played again. For a man with ten minutes a night that is a big wall. *Deflated.*
17. **Reload on the last board.** The title said 繼續終局 and it brought me back exactly to round 2. *Relieved.*
18. **Ending film.** Gorgeous, and it fits the sideways screen.
    - Turning the phone restarted it again.
    - A reload took me to 查看本局結局, which replays it.
    - At the end, 重看 and 繼續 are clear, but the final 獨孤求敗 title is cropped at the bottom of a 390 px screen.
    - The ending text is moving: 「老夫求了一世，求的原來便是這一放。」

    *Moved.*
19. **After the ending.** The title screen looks exactly as before, apart from the 查看本局結局 label. There is no mark that I finished and no record of past runs. 重看終局 is easy to find, both on the ending page and on 選境界. A new run adds 無名 as a fourth piece, but the prologue is word for word the same. *A little flat.*
20. **Wiki.** It opens in a new tab, so I can get back to my game. But its front page tells the whole twist in one sentence ("那個少年，是年輕時的他自己"), and its 進入遊戲 link opens yet another game tab. That is the same trap as entry 11. *Spoiled and wary.*

## 3. Findings

### L3-01 A second game tab silently overwrites progress made in another tab

- **Severity:** Major
- **Type:** Bug
- **Where:** any time two tabs of the game are open (the wiki's 進入遊戲 link makes one with `target=_blank`).
- **What happened:**
  - Tab 1 was in battle, round 5. I opened tab 2 and, there, won board 1 (速勝), read the post-battle story, entered the shop, won the cup game and bought 青玉佩 (gold 60 → 35).
  - I closed tab 2. Tab 1 still showed round 5 and gave no warning.
  - I ended the turn in tab 1. The save went from `result: "win"` to `result: null, phase: "enemy"`.
  - After a reload the title said 繼續第一局. The win, the gold and the item were lost.
- **What I expected:** the stale tab detects the newer save (a `storage` event or a version check) and either reloads or refuses to act.
- **Steps to reproduce:**
  1. Start board 1 in tab 1.
  2. Open the same URL in tab 2 and make progress there.
  3. Return to tab 1 and take any action.
  4. Reload.
- **Evidence:**
  - `l3/015-tab0.png` shows the stale battle after `closetab`.
  - `eval` before and after: `"5 win"` → `"5 null enemy"`.
  - After the run I briefly grepped `game/` for `storage`, `popstate`, `BroadcastChannel` and `beforeunload` listeners and found none.
- **Confidence:** Seen once, with the save change confirmed in storage.
- **Suggestion:** listen for `storage` events and reload or lock a stale tab; open 進入遊戲 in the same tab, or reuse an existing one.

### L3-03 Turning the phone mid-film restarts the film from 0:00

- **Severity:** Minor
- **Type:** Bug
- **Where:** opening film and ending film.
- **What happened:**
  - Turning to portrait swaps `prologue-film-wide.mp4` for `prologue-film.mp4` and playback starts at 0. It does the same on turning back.
  - Examples: 20.4 s → 0 (opening); 26.35 s → 0.14 s, then 0 and paused after turning back (ending).

- **Confidence:** Reproduced (four times). It is **possibly the rig**: if the local server does not support HTTP Range requests, the seek fails. Worth checking on the real host.

## 4. Verdict

**Scores:**

| | Score |
| --- | --- |
| First impression | 8/10 |
| Clarity | 6/10 |
| Comfort on a sideways phone | 6/10 |
| Desire to keep playing | 7/10 |

**Best three:**
1. Once a board has started, a reload almost always returns him to the exact place, and the title button says where.
2. The writing and the art: the ink-comic films, 韋小寶's patter and the 求敗 ending are a real treat for a 金庸 fan.
3. The post-battle story, shop and cup game survive locks, turns and reloads exactly.

**Worst three:**
1. A second tab, which the wiki's own 進入遊戲 link creates, silently wipes progress (L3-01).
2. 另開新局 replaces a run with no confirmation (L3-02).
3. The last board is unreadable at 390 px: every enemy says 主, and the arrivals pile into a clump (L3-09). Losing it means ten more boards.

**Single change that would most help 阿哲:** make the save impossible to lose by accident:
- detect a stale or second tab;
- confirm before 另開新局;
- give "continue" from the very first screen (prologue and pick).

**Would I come back tomorrow?** Yes, on the MTR. Inside a board the game keeps my place better than most phone games, and I want to see the other 金庸 heroes. But I would never again open the wiki or a second tab mid-run, and I would be very nervous near the 另開新局 button.

# Playtest report: P1 (阿芬, phone portrait, cold start)

Screenshots: `scratch/p1/` (in the findings below, `p1/NNN-*.png` means a file in that folder).

## 1. Who I was and what I covered

- **Persona:** 阿芬, 34, an office worker in Taipei. She loves 金庸's characters and plays casual phone games one-handed (right thumb) on the MRT. She has never played a tactics game, reads story text but not rules, and gives a new game about ten minutes.
- **Format:** phone portrait, 390 × 844, touch only.
- **Covered:** the front page; the title screen and its four top tools (full screen, guide, sound, settings); the whole opening film (45 s) and its end screen; the prologue story; 抓子 (choosing 3 of 5 heroes and a leader); the 第一局 story (12 lines); 軍情; 戰前整備 (all three tabs: learning a skill, slotting it, equipping armour); **the whole of board 1 (青竹渡), won on turn 7**; the post-battle story; 通吃坊 (shop plus the free 三盅 gamble); the 第二局 story, 軍情 and 整備; and the start of board 2 (神龍竹堂: deployment, then my first move).
- **Reload test:** I reloaded at the start of board 1, turn 3. Progress was kept exactly.
- **Not reached:** losing (I won the first time, so I never saw the defeat or retry flow); most of board 2; buying anything in the shop; the 群俠列傳 and 江湖誌 pages.
- **Rig trouble:** one `reload` command timed out after 30 s, but the page had in fact reloaded and worked. Enemy turns took 30 to 45 s each here, which I put down to the rig and do not report. I never had to restart the browser. Dev mode was not used.

## 2. How it felt: a play diary (阿芬's voice)

1. **Front page.** Big title 劍影江湖, one line about a dream, an old man calling himself 求敗, and heroes from different eras. That's 獨孤求敗! I'm hooked at once. Two cards: 一念江湖 ("進入遊戲") and 江湖誌. I press the first one without thinking. *Curious, pleased.*
2. **Title screen.** It's pretty. But the four little icons sit at the very top right, the one corner my thumb can't reach. I stretch and tap the guide. *Mildly awkward.*
3. **The guide.** 操作指南 is a list of 16 folded headings: 走位與地形, 出手節奏, 絕技的代價, 附加狀態, 起陣, 敵援… The two I open are dense paragraphs. I close it straight away. I'm here for 黃藥師, not homework. *Overwhelmed.*
4. **Settings.** Three tiny switches. I tap the words 棋格邊線 and nothing happens; I have to hit the small switch itself. The sound icon reads as muted, and one tap turned it on. Fine. *Neutral.*
5. **The film.** Lovely ink-painting images, a quiet mood, captions that feel like a novel. I watched all 45 seconds. My thumb rests right on 略過 in the bottom-right corner the whole time, and I'm scared I'll brush it. At the end I get 重看 / 繼續. The last caption, 「今天你我有緣，」, ends on a comma, as if cut off. *Moved, a bit nervous about the skip button.*
6. **Prologue.** The boy can't remember his name, so he becomes 無名. 求敗: 「子自己會走，自己會打。你只須告訴他們往哪裡去。」 Good, that's the whole idea in one line and I get it. But the 下一句 button moves up and down with every line, so I keep chasing it. *Delighted by the writing, annoyed by the button.*
7. **抓子 (pick heroes).** Five famous faces! But the screen is crowded: 局規, "立為領隊", "眼", 「領隊倒下，這一輪便結束」. When I tapped a hero card, the little rules box above it folded shut, the whole list jumped up, and my next tap hit a different hero. I had to work out myself that "放回" means "don't take this one". I swapped 袁承志 for 張三丰 because I love 張三丰. *Lost for a minute, then pleased with my team.*
8. **First-board story.** Each hero arrives mid-scene from his own novel: 黃藥師 crying for 蓉兒, 向問天 in chains, 張三丰 helping a kneeling monk who isn't there. Then 韋小寶 runs a gambling stall! I laughed out loud. This is the best part of the game. *Delighted.*
9. **軍情 and 整備.** A map with numbers, 支線「拒敵於外：援軍不得踏入西岸與兩座木橋」. Which side is 西? There's no compass. Then 戰前整備 with three tabs. In 武學 I see 「可參悟 3」, 淬鋒, 固本, 凝氣, a tree of tiers. What does 參悟 mean? I learned 劈空掌 because its card said 敵人無法反擊. Then I had to find 招式配置 and put it in a slot myself. I nearly put it in slot 1 over the basic attack, because slot 1 was already picked for me. *Out of my depth, but curious.*
10. **The leader.** The leader was chosen for me, and it's 黃藥師, the hero with the lowest health and defence (102 / 9). If he falls, I lose. Nobody warned me that was a risky choice. *Uneasy, only in hindsight.*
11. **Battle, turn 1.** A beautiful little 3D river crossing, but it fills only the top half of the screen. The bottom half, where my thumb lives, is water and buttons. A hint said to tap heroes to swap places, then 「開戰」, and it vanished after my first tap. Nothing told me what to do once the fight began. I worked out "tap my hero, then tap a glowing square" by trying it. *Tense.*
12. **First attack.** I tapped my hero next to an enemy and got a nice bar of moves (武當綿掌 / 羅漢拳 / 撤步). Tapping the enemy opened 交鋒預覽, with health before and after, damage, and 「敵方還擊 −4」. That's really clear! I pressed 確認出招 and saw a big floating −4. *Proud. This is the moment it clicked.*
13. **But "next to" isn't next to.** Three or four times, my hero stood right beside an enemy on screen, diagonally, and tapping the enemy only opened its profile card (敵情查探). No "too far" message. I thought the game was broken until I guessed that diagonal squares don't count. *Confused and a bit cross.*
14. **撤步 saved me.** I put 張三丰 in a bad spot and found 撤步. He stepped back with a little 「移動已撤回」 message. Lovely. But my chosen move quietly switched back to the basic attack, and I used the weaker one without noticing. *Relieved, then a bit cheated.*
15. **Enemy turn.** Things happen fast. Someone poisons 黃藥師 (a 毒 badge appears), a 縛 badge appears on 張三丰, reinforcements arrive. There was no explanation unless I dug into 選單 → 紀錄, which is actually a good log, but I'd never have found it alone. Levelling up happened silently: Lv.1 became Lv.2 with no fanfare. *Overwhelmed but excited.*
16. **The reload test.** I "closed" the game, as if a LINE message came in, and came back. The title now said 繼續第一局, and one tap put me back on turn 3, exactly where I was. *Very reassured.*
17. **Tapping enemies again.** Near the end I tapped the boss's head and got his profile card again. When I tapped his feet instead, I got the attack preview. So you must tap the base of a figure, not the figure. My thumb naturally goes for the body. *Frustrated.*
18. **A crate.** I tried to walk onto a tea crate and a calm message said 「渡口茶箱 · 堆疊的貨箱封住此格，須繞道。」 That's exactly the kind of help I want. *Pleased.*
19. **Victory, sort of.** I ended turn 7 with the boss at 2 health. During the enemy's turn the screen simply cut to 「戰後 · 勝」 and story text. I never saw who struck the winning blow, and there was no victory moment, no fanfare and no 勝 banner on the board. Then 韋小寶 said 「這一口你沒押中」: I had failed the side bet, but nothing said why. *Glad, but cheated of the moment.*
20. **The shop.** 韋小寶's 通吃坊 is gorgeous: painted item cards and prices in 兩. The free 三盅 shell game is charming (I won a 青鋒劍). *Delighted.*
21. **Board 2.** The new hero 張無忌 (「咦……不痛了？」) is a lovely touch. The setup screen now shows 可參悟 6 or 7 on everyone. I never learned how to spend those properly, so that's homework stacking up. On the new board, my first tap moved 黃藥師 straight away, because he was already selected and I hadn't noticed. *Charmed by the story, tired of the system.*

## 3. Findings

### P1-01: Tapping an enemy's body or head opens its profile card instead of attacking it
- **Severity:** Major
- **Type:** Touch or pointer / Feedback
- **Where:** board 1, targeting an enemy after choosing a move.
- **What happened:** On turn 7, with 向問天 next to 余滄海 and 吸功入地小法 chosen (red target reticle showing on the boss, `p1/102-t7-b.png`), tapping the boss's upper figure at (287,272) opened 敵情查探. Tapping his base at (287,290) opened 交鋒預覽. The same happened on turn 6. Earlier attempts on 鳳天南 also opened the card. A thumb aims at the figure, not at the floor under it.
- **What I expected:** while a move is chosen, tapping anywhere on a targetable enemy (figure, health bar or tile) should open the attack preview.
- **Steps to reproduce:** choose a hero next to an enemy → choose a move → tap the top half of the enemy figure.
- **Evidence:** `p1/102-t7-b.png`, text dumps (the 敵情查探 card at 287,272; 交鋒預覽 at 287,290).
- **Confidence:** Seen twice (the boss on turns 6 and 7).
- **Suggestion:** make the figure's hit area resolve to its tile while targeting.

### P1-02: Diagonal neighbours look adjacent, and an out-of-range tap gives no "out of range" message
- **Severity:** Major
- **Type:** Understanding / Feedback
- **Where:** board 1, all attacks.
- **What happened:** In the tilted 3D view, an enemy one square away diagonally looks like a neighbour. Tapping it with a 1-square move just opened the enemy's profile card, and nothing said it was too far. This happened with the scout (turn 1), the archer (turn 2), 鳳天南 (turn 5), the 棍徒 (turn 6) and the boss (turn 5, `p1/094-t5-k.png`). Nothing anywhere says that range is counted in straight lines.

- **Steps to reproduce:** move a hero diagonally next to an enemy → choose a 1-square move → tap the enemy.
- **Evidence:** `p1/036-sel-zsf.png`, `p1/087-t5-d.png`, `p1/094-t5-k.png`.
- **Confidence:** Reproduced.

## 4. Verdict

| | Score (out of 10) |
| --- | --- |
| First impression | 8 |
| Clarity | 4 |

**Three best things**
1. The story: famous characters pulled out mid-scene from their own novels, with 韋小寶 as the bookie. That alone makes 阿芬 want to see the next board.
2. The 交鋒預覽 attack preview, plus 撤步 undo: they make a scary genre feel safe.
3. The art and presentation: the ink-wash film, the 3D river crossing, the shop cards. And the save and resume works perfectly.

**Three worst things**
1. No guided first battle; the rules are a 16-part manual.
2. Targeting: you have to tap the base of a figure rather than its body, diagonal squares look adjacent but don't count, and neither problem gets a "too far" message.
3. The one-handed layout: the board sits high, the tools are top-right, small switches, buttons that move (下一句, the move tray), and 10 to 11 px text.

**The single change that would most help 阿芬:** a short guided first turn on 青竹渡 (tap 張三丰 → tap this square → tap that enemy → read the preview → confirm → end the turn), with "too far" feedback and whole-figure tap targets.

**Would she come back tomorrow?** Probably yes, for the characters: she wants to meet 令狐冲 and to know what 求敗 is. But she nearly closed the tab during 整備 (參悟, 招式配置) and again when her taps on enemies "did nothing". If board 2 piles on more systems without teaching them, she drops off there.

# L1 playtest report: 劍影江湖, phone landscape (844 × 390, touch)

Screenshots: `scratch/l1/NNN-l1-*.png` (shortened below to `l1/NNN`).

## 1. Who I was and what I covered

- **Persona:** 小凱, 19, a university student in Taichung. Plays 原神 / 明日方舟 / 星穹鐵道 every day with the phone sideways and his thumbs in the bottom corners. Skips story, taps fast, has read no 金庸, knows tactics-game basics from 明日方舟 but has never played grid turn tactics.
- **Format:** phone landscape, 844 × 390 CSS, touch only. No `?dev`.
- **Covered:** front page → title screen and its tools (full screen, sound, settings, guide) → opening film (watched about 15 s, then skipped) → prologue dialogue → 抓子 hero pick → pre-battle dialogue → 軍情 briefing → 戰前整備 (陣容 and 武學, spent skill points) → **Board 1 青竹渡, played turn by turn to a win on turn 9** (the side objective failed) → a `reload` on turn 2 → camera tools, guide, menu → post-battle 通吃坊 (cup game, a purchase) → board 2 story, briefing and preparation → the first turn of **board 2 紅楓驛**.
- **Not reached:** losing and retrying (I never lost), most of board 2, the 裝備物資 tab, 群俠列傳, the wiki.
- **Rig trouble:** `ctl.mjs` hung past the 120 s timeout three times on `drag` / `pinch` commands. Each time the action had still happened in the page (the screenshots were taken), and the browser kept working without a restart. I count this as a rig problem, not a game bug.

## 3. Findings

**L1-01: Tapping outside a story dialog closes the whole prologue or pre-battle story and drops you to the title screen**
- **Severity:** Major
- **Type:** Bug / Flow
- **Where:** The prologue 「入夢」 dialog after skipping the film; the pre-battle 「第一局 · 青竹渡 臨陣」 dialog.
- **What happened:** A tap on the dim backdrop outside the dialog box (for example where the film's 略過 button had just been, at 794,356, or above the box at 422,60) closes the dialog **and the whole sequence**, back to the title. From the prologue, pressing 入夢 again replays the 45 s film from the start. From the battle story it shows 「繼續第一局」.
- **Expected:** A backdrop tap does nothing on story dialogs, or advances one line.
- **Steps:** Title → 入夢 → 略過 → as soon as the dialog appears, tap 794,356 (below the dialog). Or, at the 臨陣 dialog, tap 422,60.
- **Evidence:** l1/009, l1/010, l1/018. Seen 3 times.
- **Confidence:** Reproduced
- **Suggestion:** Make story dialogs modal, with no dismiss on backdrop taps.

**L1-02: Choosing a skill after a "stay in place" with no enemy in range leaves the hero stuck: he cannot move, undo or cancel**
- **Severity:** Major
- **Type:** Bug / Flow
- **Where:** Board 1, turns 3, 6 and 8 (張三丰, and 胡斐 on turn 6).
- **What happened:** Tap a hero (selected), tap him again (the skill panel opens with no 撤步, so it reads as "act from here"), pick 羅漢拳 → top toast 「羅漢拳 · 47 · 選擇範圍內的敵人」 but no enemy is in the red tiles. There is no cancel. Tapping a green move tile does nothing. Tapping empty ground and then the hero again returns to the same targeting state with the panel. The only way out is 結束回合, which wastes that hero's turn.
- **Expected:** A cancel / back on the skill panel, or the move range still usable until the hero acts.
- **Steps:** On your turn, tap 張三丰's base twice, choose 羅漢拳 with no enemy orthogonally adjacent, then try to move him.
- **Evidence:** l1/083–088, l1/096–100, l1/113–126.
- **Confidence:** Reproduced
- **Suggestion:** Add a 「取消」 to the skill panel, and treat a second tap on the hero as "open the panel", not "commit to staying".

**L1-03: Adjacency on the 45°-rotated grid is never explained, and tapping an out-of-range enemy silently opens its info card**
- **Severity:** Major
- **Type:** Understanding / Feedback
- **Where:** All battles, every camera mode, 俯視 included.
- **What happened:** Enemies that sit right beside your hero on screen are diagonal on the grid and cannot be hit. With a skill armed, tapping them opens 「敵情查探」 rather than saying "out of range". I failed this about eight times before working out the rule. 俯視 is still rotated 45°, so the grid never lines up with the screen.
- **Expected:** A first-battle tutorial that shows a valid attack tile, plus a toast like 「目標不在招式範圍內」. Ideally an axis-aligned top-down view.
- **Evidence:** l1/036–037, l1/072, l1/084, l1/092, l1/117, and repeated 敵情查探 text dumps.
- **Confidence:** Reproduced
- **Suggestion:** When a skill is armed, highlight the hittable enemies themselves, not only red tiles.

## 4. Verdict

| Measure | Score / 10 |
| --- | --- |
| First impression | 7 |
| Clarity | 3 |
| Comfort on this format | 5 |
| Desire to keep playing | 4 |

**Three best things**
1. Gorgeous diorama boards (青竹渡's bridge and lotus water, 紅楓驛's maples) and beautiful painted portraits.
2. The 交鋒預覽 combat preview: HP → HP, counter damage and effects, all clear at a glance.
3. Saving is solid: auto-save, 繼續第一局, and an exact resume after reload. Fast taps rarely double-fire.

**Three worst things**
1. Grid adjacency on a 45°-rotated board is never taught, and attacking a diagonal enemy fails silently by opening an info card.
2. A hero can get stuck in skill targeting with no cancel (L1-02), and a backdrop tap throws you out of the story to the title screen (L1-01).
3. No show-by-doing tutorial; the help is long text panels.

**The single change that would help 小凱 most:** a 60-second guided first turn on 青竹渡 that makes him tap his hero, a highlighted tile and a highlighted hittable enemy (with a visible 取消), so he learns the diagonal-free adjacency by doing it once.

**Would I come back tomorrow?** Maybe. The art and the 交鋒預覽 made me want to like it, and I did win. But I spent half of board 1 confused about why my attacks "didn't work", and three times the game dumped me to the title. If a friend told me "attacks only go up-left, up-right, down-left, down-right on screen", I'd give board 2 a go; on my own I'd probably drop it.

**Source peeking:** none. I did not read the repository; every cause given above is inferred from the screen and `eval` reads.

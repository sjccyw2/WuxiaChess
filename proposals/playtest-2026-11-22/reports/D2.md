# D2 playtest report: 劍影江湖 (desktop 1440 × 900, keyboard + mouse)

Screenshots: `scratch/d2/NNN-name.png` (shown below as `d2/NNN`).

## 1. Who I was and what I covered

**Persona:** Iris (林怡君), 29, streams strategy and roguelike games from Taipei. She plays fast with one hand on the keyboard, looks for shortcuts first, wants every number to be inspectable, and needs text her viewers can read at a glance.

**Format:** desktop, 1440 × 900, mouse and keyboard, port 9308.

**What I played:**
- New game: the opening film, the prologue story, picking heroes (抓子) and choosing a leader, all tried from the keyboard first.
- The guide (操作指南) and its key list.
- **Board 1, 青竹渡:** briefing, 整備 and placement, then **4 full rounds played honestly**. I tried every listed key and every key I would guess. Then I finished the board with dev 速勝・支線.
- **After the win:** the story, then **通吃坊** in full: shelves, item details, buying, the 三盅通吃 cup game, and the team page inside the shop.
- **整備 / 隊伍 in depth:** 陣容 with drag and drop and changing the leader; 武學 with the tree, learning, loadout slots, 授業武譜 and 合招譜; 裝備物資 with equipping by click and by drag.
- **Board 2, 蘆花汀:** 4 honest rounds with a newly learned art and the bought sword, then 速勝.
- **Board 3, 神龍竹堂:** 3 honest rounds with new arts, medicine used mid-battle, reinforcements arriving. Then I lost on purpose with 速敗 and followed the whole loss flow to the 眼破 summary.
- **試局 (special rules):**
  - 空山琴臺 (guard the zither): 3 rounds.
  - 無刻漏臺 (break the posts): 3 rounds.
  - 倒影天街 (two bosses): 3 rounds, plus 1 more round in `?classic`.
  - My play on these boards was mostly positional. I spent the time reading information, not optimising damage.
- **Reloads and back:** reloaded mid-battle, in the shop, on the shop's team page and in 整備; used browser `back` from the shop.
- **`?classic`:** one round, then back to the normal address.

**Not reached:**
- Boards 4 to 10 in a normal run (I only saw 4, 9 and 10 through 試局).
- The final board and its film.
- The 襄陽殘垣 guard board (I used 空山琴臺 instead).

**Rig trouble:** none. The browser never needed a restart.

**Source reading:** after playing I spent about five minutes reading the source, only to pin down one 404 (P-D2-27). I say so in that entry.

## 2. How it felt: a play diary

1. **Title screen, Tab first.** I hit Tab expecting to land on 入夢. Instead focus went 江湖誌 → fullscreen → 遊玩指南 → sound → settings, and only then to 入夢. The gold focus ring is clear, though. *Mildly annoyed; good that focus is visible.*
2. **Opening film.** Space skipped it at once, and there was a 略過 button. *Pleased.*
3. **Prologue dialogue.** I pressed Space to go to the next line, and it skipped the whole prologue: focus had been on 略過, not 下一句. On the very next story the focus was on 下一句. *Confused. I lost the story I wanted to read and had to guess which button had focus.*
4. **Esc in a story** threw me back to the title screen instead of closing the dialogue. Luckily 繼續第一局 resumed on the same line, 4/12. *Startled, then relieved.*
5. **抓子 (pick 3 of 5).** I could do it all with Tab and Enter, which I liked. But the cards show no numbers at all, and I only see a leader's effect after I make that hero leader. 楊過 turned out to have 184 HP against everyone else's 70. On a wide screen this is a narrow 740 px box that has to scroll. *Frustrated: I want to compare builds on stream.*
6. **The guide.** The key list is the very last thing in the guide, at 11 px, under 16 collapsed sections. The keys themselves are good: 1–4, G, [ ], E, Esc, ?, D, Tab. *Found it, squinting.*
7. **Placement.** Tab cycled heroes, D showed enemy range, G hid the grid. Enter started the battle straight away with no confirmation. *OK, fast. I like fast.*
8. **First enemy check.** Hovering an enemy shows nothing. Clicking it opens a modal 敵情查探 with good data: stats, the special move, a "蓄勢" countdown and the AI rule (坐鎮). But it's a modal that covers the board. *Happy with the data, annoyed it isn't a hover card.*
9. **The 交鋒預覽 forecast** is excellent: HP before → after for both sides, the counterattack, the cost, the weapon traits. Then I pressed Enter to confirm and nothing happened. Space did nothing either. I had to click 確認出招. *Delighted, then deflated.*
10. **Pressing E to end the turn.** A confirmation came up ("尚有 1 位俠客可行動"). Focus sat on the X, so Enter *closed* the dialogue. I needed Tab ×3 and then Enter. *This is the moment I said "who designed this?" on stream.*
11. **Enemy turn.** Around 20–25 seconds of watching on this rig, with no speed-up and no skip. Space did nothing, and Settings only offers 「完整動作與特效」 on or off. *Bored. Chat would be bored.*
12. **Battle log (選單 → 紀錄).** It has filters by type and lists every hit with its damage. *Genuinely delighted: proper information.*
13. **Learning 纏字訣 mid-battle** from 隊伍 → 武學, then pressing 3 on the board to use it. The status 纏械 showed on the enemy's info. *Felt like a real build moment.*
14. **Board 2 adjacency.** On the rotated isometric board I clicked an enemy three times that *looked* adjacent. Each time the game silently opened the enemy info modal instead of saying "out of range". I finally worked out that the 1-tile range is orthogonal only. *Annoyed. It felt like a hidden rule.*
15. **通吃坊.** The cup game was cute and worked from the keyboard. Buying is one click with no undo. The item details are an accordion with one open at a time, so I can't compare two swords side by side. *Mixed.*
16. **Equipment.** Drag and drop worked nicely. Clicking an item shows "攻擊 ＋10" but not what my attack becomes, and not what I'd lose against the weapon I have on. *Wanted a comparison.*
17. **Loadout surprise.** After learning 全真劍法 I pressed 「配置此格」. It replaced 蛤蟆功 in slot 1 while slots 2 and 3 were empty. *Annoyed: I had to fix it by hand.*
18. **Granting a manual.** I granted 《綿掌》 to 楊過. Only afterwards did the game tell me it needs 固本一重 first, and manuals are one per hero for good. *Felt tricked.*
19. **Reinforcement markers (援)** on tiles, a hover label at the bottom ("敵援入場處 · 第三回合 · 神龍教青龍門眾兩名") and the header line "敵援 · 下回合 2 名". *Very clear. Good.*
20. **Losing.** The defeat story, then a shop where I could still buy for the next run, then 「結束此行」, then the 眼破 summary with stats and a moody vulture picture. *Clear and a bit poignant.*
21. **The guard board.** The header line "守 · 尚須 6 回合", the zither's HP, and log lines like 「七弦琴氣血過半」. *Clear.* The post-breaking board has no equivalent "破 0/2" counter. *Inconsistent.*
22. **`?classic`.** A totally different HUD (移動/招式/待命 buttons). D and Tab and number keys all did nothing, and pieces piled on top of each other. *Felt like a different, older game.*

## 3. Findings

### P-D2-01 · E opens an end-turn confirmation whose focus is on Close, so Enter cancels
- **Severity:** Major
- **Type:** Flow
- **Where:** Battle, pressing E (結束回合) while any hero can still act.
- **What happened:** E opens 「結束我方回合 · 尚有 N 位俠客可行動」. Initial focus is on the X (關閉視窗). Enter closes the dialogue, which is the opposite of what a keyboard player means. Pressing E again inside the dialogue does nothing. The order is X → message text → 返回戰場 → 結束回合, so ending a turn from the keyboard takes E, Tab, Tab, Tab, Enter.
- **What I expected:** Focus on 結束回合, or E/Enter confirming.
- **Steps:** Start any battle, press E, press Enter. The dialogue closes and the turn does not end.
- **Evidence:** `d2/048`. eval of `document.activeElement` gave 關閉視窗, then 「尚有 1 位俠客 可行動。」, then 返回戰場.
- **Confidence:** Reproduced (rounds 1–3 of board 1, and later boards).
- **Suggestion:** Focus the primary button, and let a second E confirm.

### P-D2-02 · Enter and Space do not confirm 交鋒預覽
- **Severity:** Major
- **Type:** Flow
- **Where:** Battle, the attack forecast after picking a target.
- **What happened:** Focus is on an unlabeled button (the X). Enter and Space close the preview instead of confirming. Every attack needs a mouse click on 確認出招.
- **What I expected:** Enter confirms. It is the most-repeated action in the game.
- **Steps:** Select a hero, press 1, click an enemy in range, press Enter.
- **Evidence:** `d2/040`. eval returned a BUTTON with empty text. Pressing Enter (and in another try Space) left the turn unchanged with no attack made.
- **Confidence:** Reproduced.

### P-D2-03 · Enemy turns cannot be sped up or skipped
- **Severity:** Major for my persona (streaming); Minor in general
- **Type:** Flow
- **Where:** Every enemy phase, and the 「第 N 回合 · 我方行動」 banner.
- **What happened:** Each enemy walks and attacks in turn. Nothing speeds it up: no Space, no click, no speed option in 遊戲設定. The only setting is 「完整動作與特效」 on or off. With 8 or more enemies it is a long wait every round. (The absolute length here is rig-inflated, but the lack of any skip is by design.)
- **What I expected:** Hold Space or click to fast-forward, or a 1×/2×/4× setting.
- **Evidence:** `d2/049`–`053`, `d2/093`–`095`, `d2/096` (settings).
- **Confidence:** Reproduced.

## 4. Verdict

| | Score /10 |
|---|---|
| First impression | 7 |
| Clarity | 6 |
| Comfort on this format (desktop, keyboard and mouse) | 5 |
| Desire to keep playing | 7 |

**Three best things**
1. The attack forecast and the enemy info. Real numbers, the counterattack, enemy intent and AI rules. This is Into the Breach-grade honesty once you click.
2. Build depth: per-hero trees, new arts learned between and even during fights, combo hints (合招譜), weapon traits like 收隙 and 走勁.
3. A filterable battle log and clear reinforcement warnings. Rare and very welcome.

**Three worst things**
1. Keyboard confirm paths are broken where they matter most. Enter cancels end-turn and cannot confirm an attack; Space in a story skips the whole scene.
2. No way to speed up or skip enemy turns and banners.
3. Information you must *click* for, inside modals that cover the board: no hover cards, no out-of-range feedback, no side-by-side comparison anywhere on a 1440 px screen.

**The single change that would most help me:** make Enter/Space always confirm the primary action (end turn, 確認出招, next line), and add a hold-to-fast-forward for enemy turns.

**Would I come back tomorrow?** Yes, probably on stream. The tactical layer and build choices are interesting enough to theorycraft about, and the information is there once you dig. But I'd warn chat that I'm playing mouse-heavy. I'd want the keyboard confirms and a speed toggle before I'd run a full ten-board run live.

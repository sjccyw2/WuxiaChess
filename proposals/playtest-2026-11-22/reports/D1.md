# Playtest report D1: 老王, desktop 1440 × 900, mouse first

Screenshots: `scratch/d1/` (paths below give the file name only; they all live in that folder).

## 1. Who I was and what I covered

- **Persona:** 老王 (王建國), 45, an accountant in Taipei. He grew up on DOS-era 金庸群俠傳, 仙劍 and 三國志英傑傳, uses the mouse for everything, and expects hover tooltips, right-click to cancel, the wheel to zoom and Esc to open a menu.
- **Format:** desktop, 1440 × 900, on port 9307. Developer mode was never used.
- **Covered:**
  - the front page;
  - the title screen and its four tools (full screen, guide, sound, settings) plus 群俠列傳;
  - the opening film, with mouse, Space and Esc;
  - the prologue and the draft (抓子);
  - the board-1 briefing, 軍情 and 戰前整備 (all three tabs: learning skills, equipping them, items);
  - **board 1, 第一局 青竹渡, played from the first move to a win on round 7**, with a page reload in the middle of round 3;
  - the post-battle story, the 通吃坊 shop and the 三盅通吃 cup game;
  - the board-2 story, 軍情 and preparation;
  - **board 2, 紅楓驛, rounds 1 and 2**, where I tried the keyboard shortcuts.
- **Not reached:** the second half of board 2. I never lost a battle, so I did not see the defeat or retry flow.
- **Rig trouble:** one long chained command (drag, then right-click, then Esc) went past the 120-second timeout and finished in the background. The browser itself never needed a restart. Native `title=` tooltips do not show in the rig's screenshots, so I checked tooltips by reading the attributes in the page.
- **Source:** I did not read any source code.

## 2. How it felt: a play diary (老王's voice)

1. **Front page.** A big 劍影江湖 title and two cards, 「遊戲 一念江湖」 and 「圖鑑 江湖誌」. I can tell one is the game and one is a reference book, so I click the left one. But the game card is called 一念江湖, not 劍影江湖. Then the game's own corner says 武俠戰棋. That is three names in two screens. *Mildly puzzled.*
2. **Title screen.** For three to six seconds there is only a logo on a dark background and nothing to click. Then the 入夢 and 群俠列傳 buttons appear. The small icons at the top right have no words on them. When I hovered, the browser's own little yellow tooltip showed eventually, which was a relief. *OK.*
3. **The guide (遊玩指南).** Sixteen folded headings with a lot of dense text. It explains rules well, but it has no list of keys at all. *I'll read it later, maybe.*
4. **Settings.** Three switches: sound, tile edges, full effects. The small grey explanations under them are 12 px, hard on my eyes. Right-clicking the box did not close it, but Esc and clicking outside did. *Fine.*
5. **入夢 and the film.** The film is lovely, and it fills my wide screen edge to edge. I wanted to pause it with Space, as in any video player. Instead Space **skipped the whole film** at about 37 seconds and dropped me into the dialogue, so I never saw the ending. There is no pause button. *Annoyed: I wanted to watch it.*
6. **Prologue dialogue.** I clicked on the text itself to go on, the way 仙劍 works, and nothing happened. Only the 「下一句」 button advances. Enter and Space work only once that button has keyboard focus, and at the very start the focus sits on 「略過」, so pressing Space there would skip everything. The writing is good: 「我叫……我叫……」 and the name 「無名」. But the boy asks 「老伯，你又是誰？」 and the old man never answers. *Charmed by the text, irritated by the clicking.*
7. **抓子 (the draft).** I am dealt five white stones and keep three. The names wrap badly in the narrow cards (黃藥/師, 張三/丰, 洪七/公). The 「立為領隊」 buttons are hidden below the fold, and I only found them by scrolling. 「利劍境界」 and 「臺邊的五行字 0／5 行」 are never explained. I swapped 張三丰 for 洪七公 because 降龍十八掌 is a must. *Excited, though the layout is cramped.*
8. **Briefing (臨陣).** Each line fades in for about a second, and the buttons jump up and down as the box resizes, so my mouse keeps missing 「下一句」. The characters sound right. 黃藥師's 「我黃老邪要哭便哭，要唱便唱」 is pure 黃藥師, and 韋小寶's 「辣塊媽媽」 and 「玉皇大帝是我姐夫」 made me laugh out loud. *Delighted.*
9. **戰前整備.** The skill trees look serious. To learn a skill I had to scroll the narrow right panel to find 「修習」 at the very bottom. A newly learned move does not go into my move slots on its own: I had to find a second sub-tab, 招式配置, choose an empty slot, then press 「配置到第二格」. That is three hidden steps. One description reads 「懷中抱月取作胡家刀法的一式名，讀作誘敵的虛招，是本作的讀法」. 「是本作的讀法」 is a designer's note, not something a player should read. *Feeling the bureaucracy.*
10. **The X button on 整備.** I pressed the X in the corner to close what I thought was a sub-panel. It dropped me on the title screen (「繼續整備」), as if I had quit. *Startled.*
11. **The first board.** Beautiful: a stone bridge, a pavilion, bamboo, a little boat. I hovered over an enemy and got nothing: no name, no health, and the pointer is a grabbing hand, not a pointing finger. Clicking the enemy opened a good 敵情查探 panel. Hovering over a floor tile showed my route and a small label at the bottom centre, 「渡口石坪 · 5 步」. That label is good, but it sits far from my mouse. *Hmm, half of what I expect.*
12. **Wheel and drag.** The wheel zooms and dragging rotates the board, just as I hoped. The guide says Shift-drag pans, but the screen never says so. *Good.*
13. **First attack.** After the move, a 「出招」 list pops up beside the hero and shows damage numbers. Choosing a target opens 「交鋒預覽」 with exact HP before and after and the counter-attack. That is better than 英傑傳 ever was. 胡家刀法 left a 「隙」 mark on the enemy, but hovering the mark explains nothing, and there are 主, 副, 毒 and 破 marks too. *Proud of the hit, curious about the marks.*
14. **Right-click.** I moved 黃藥師 to the wrong tile and right-clicked to take it back. Nothing happened. The 「撤步」 (undo move) button exists, but it sits at the bottom of the skill pop-up, where I had to scroll inside the pop-up to see it. Later I pressed Esc after a move and it **silently put the hero back**, with no message. *Lost, then surprised.*
15. **Out of range.** I clicked an enemy that was just outside the range of the move I had chosen. Instead of a clear 'cannot reach' mark on the board, the full enemy information panel opened, with a small grey message at the bottom: 「目標在招式範圍外，可先移動或查探敵情」. This happened four times. Twice the reason was a diagonal: I could not tell from the board that diagonal squares do not count as next to me. *Annoyed.*
16. **Enemy reinforcements.** A small 「敵援 · 下回合 3 名」 under the round counter warned me. Nice. Then 鳳天南 arrived. I know him as a 飛狐 villain, yet he is not on 胡斐's board as a nemesis; he is only a 副手. Nobody comments on it. *A missed chance, but fine.*
17. **Closing the lid (reload) in round 3.** I came back to the title screen with 「繼續第一局」. One click brought me back to round 3 with everything as it was. The camera angle had changed, though. *Relieved.*
18. **Ending the turn.** 「結束回合」 asks for confirmation when someone has not acted: 「尚有 1 位俠客可行動」. Enter did not confirm it. Sometimes it did not ask at all. *A bit inconsistent.*
19. **Victory on round 7.** 洪七公's 神龍擺尾 took 余滄海 from 29 to 0. The game went straight into the after-battle story. There was no 'victory' page, no tally of experience gained, no note that the side quest 拒敵於外 had succeeded. 韋小寶 does mention the reward in passing. The story is excellent: 木高峰's 「駝子可沒答應替人打這一架！」, and 阿青 appearing with 「范蠡呢？……可見到我的羊麼？」, straight from 越女劍. *Very happy.*
20. **通吃坊 and 三盅通吃.** The shop is a narrow window in the middle of my wide screen, and I had to scroll it. The cup game is cute 韋小寶 business. The same whip is called 錦紋軟鞭 in its title and 錦紋長鞭 in its description. *Amused.*
21. **Board 2, 紅楓驛.** The autumn colours are gorgeous. The side quest 「由胡斐給田歸農最後一擊」 is perfect for 胡斐, a man with a feud against 田歸農. I learned that pressing `e` starts the battle and also ends the turn, `2` picks the second move, and `?` or `h` opens the guide. Enter does **not** confirm an attack preview. None of these keys appear anywhere on screen. *Pleased to find them, cross that I had to guess.*

## 3. Findings

### D1-01 Space skips the whole opening film instead of pausing it; the film cannot be paused
- **Severity:** Minor
- **Type:** Flow
- **Where:** the opening film after 入夢
- **What happened:** pressing Space at about 37 of 45 seconds ended the film and opened the 入夢 dialogue at once. The film has no pause control; the only controls are 「略過」 and 「關閉聲音」.
- **What I expected:** Space pauses and resumes the film, as in any video player, with only Esc or 「略過」 skipping it.
- **Steps to reproduce:** click 入夢, wait about 10 s, press Space.
- **Evidence:** `014-d1-11-film-b.png` (the film at 37.3 s), `015-d1-12-film-space.png` (the dialogue straight after Space). The focus had moved to 「略過」 (checked with `document.activeElement`), so Space activated that button.
- **Confidence:** Seen once
- **Suggestion:** do not put the keyboard focus on 「略過」 during the film; give Space to pause and keep Esc for skipping.

### D1-02 The keyboard focus starts on 「略過」 in every dialogue, so Space or Enter skips the whole scene
- **Severity:** Major
- **Type:** Flow
- **Where:** the prologue, 臨陣 and 戰後 dialogues
- **What happened:**
  - When the prologue opened, `activeElement` was the 「略過」 button (prologue-skip), so the first Space or Enter skips the scene.
  - Clicking the text panel does not advance the dialogue, and after such a click Enter and Space do nothing.
  - Only after clicking 「下一句」 do Enter and Space advance line by line.
- **What I expected:** clicking anywhere on the dialogue box advances, as in 仙劍, and Enter and Space always mean 'next line'.
- **Steps to reproduce:** start a new game and let the film end or skip it; check the focus. Then click the text body and press Enter: nothing happens.
- **Evidence:** the eval result `<button class="mj-action" data-do="prologue-skip">略過</button>`; the text was unchanged after clicking at (720,450) and after Enter and Space (log at prologue 2/4).
- **Confidence:** Reproduced
- **Suggestion:** focus 「下一句」 by default and make a click anywhere on the dialogue box advance.

### D1-03 Dialogue buttons move every line, so the mouse misses 「下一句」
- **Severity:** Minor
- **Type:** Layout
- **Where:** every dialogue box
- **What happened:** the box changes height with each line. 「下一句」 jumps between y≈604 and y≈629, and the box top between 251 and 275. Each line also fades in for about a second, during which clicks are lost.
- **What I expected:** the button stays in one place.
- **Steps to reproduce:** advance the 臨陣 dialogue and watch where 「下一句」 sits in `text`.
- **Evidence:** the `text` output shows 「下一句」 at y 604, 605, 618 and 629 on different lines; `023-d1-20-yu.png`.
- **Confidence:** Reproduced
- **Suggestion:** give the box a fixed height, or pin the button row to the bottom.

### D1-07 Hovering over pieces, tiles' contents and status marks explains nothing; the board pointer is always a grabbing hand
- **Severity:** Major for this persona
- **Type:** Touch or pointer / Understanding
- **Where:** every battle
- **What happened:**
  - Hovering over an enemy or an ally shows no name, health or intent. Only floor tiles get a label, a small line at the bottom centre such as 「渡口石坪 · 5 步」.
  - The status marks above heads (隙, 破, 毒, 主, 副, 援) are about 10 px tall and have no hover text.

- **Steps to reproduce:** in battle, hover an enemy and wait 1.5 s; hover the 「隙」 mark.

**Scores out of 10**

**The three best things**
1. Story text that respects the novels, funny and in character.
2. The attack preview with exact numbers, plus the route-and-steps preview on hover.
3. Lovely 3D boards, and progress that survives closing the laptop.

**The three worst things**
1. Hovering a piece or a status mark tells me nothing, and right-click does nothing, so as a PC player I am guessing.
2. Dialogue and confirmation handling: clicking the text does not advance, the focus sits on 略過 so Space skips scenes and the film, and Enter does not confirm attacks or end-turn.
3. Hidden steps in preparation (learn, then a separate equip tab, with the button below the fold) and a cramped centre-column layout on a wide screen.

**The single change that would help 老王 most:** a hover card on every piece and every status mark (name, HP, mark meanings, 'in range / out of range' for the chosen move), together with right-click as cancel or undo.

**Would he come back tomorrow?** Yes. He wants to see which 金庸 heroes 求敗 lends next and what 韋小寶 says. But he will grumble every lunch hour about clicking the 「下一句」 button and hunting for 撤步.

# L2 playtest report: Mei (美玲), phone landscape 844 × 390, touch only

## 1. Who I was and what I covered

**Persona:** Mei, 31, a nurse in Kuala Lumpur. She grew up on the TVB 金庸 dramas, loves 小龍女 and 黃蓉, reads Traditional characters slowly, has never played a tactics game, plays in short breaks with the phone held sideways.

**Format:** phone landscape, 844 × 390 CSS px, touch only. Browser on port 9305. No restarts were needed.

**What I played:**
- New game: watched about 5 s of the opening film, skipped it, read the 4 序幕 pages, picked heroes in 抓子 (swapped 張無忌 out for 小龍女 and made her leader).
- Board 1 青竹渡: read 軍情, used all of 整備 (learned 玉蜂針, equipped it, put on 玄鐵護腕, read about 金創藥), then played **3 honest rounds**. During those rounds I took back moves (撤步), opened the enemy info, turned on the threat display and the grid, used a 金創藥 from 藥囊, opened the goals page and **reloaded in the middle of the battle**. I finished the board with 速勝・支線.
- After the win: read the post-battle talk (9 pages), played the 三盅通吃 cup game, read every shop section, bought 踏雲靴 and 玉蜂漿, **reloaded inside the shop**, then went through 整理隊伍: dragged to reorder, changed the leader, gave out the 《綿掌》 manual, learned 固本 and 綿掌 and equipped it.
- Board 2 紅楓驛: tried the camera panel (俯視, zoom), opened the team page mid-battle, played **3 honest rounds** with 綿掌, 玉蜂針 and 程靈素's healing art 藥王方脈, used the threat display, then 速勝.
- Board 3 神龍竹堂: swapped starting positions, played **3 honest rounds**, opened the menu, 指南 (guide) and 紀錄 (log), used 任盈盈's self-heal 清心普善咒, then **速敗** to follow the defeat flow through to 眼破 and the main screen.
- 試局 on **空山琴臺** (guard the 七弦琴): read the briefing, inspected the qin, played **3 honest rounds**.

**What I did not reach:** a 夜話 (night talk). None came up in boards 1 to 3, and I lost on board 3. I did not try 群俠列傳, 設定 or the sound toggle in depth. I made no purchase that the cup game had already given me for free.

**Rig trouble:** none. Taps that seemed to miss were checked with `els` or a grid shot. Those are covered in L2-08 and L2-09.

## 2. How it felt: a play diary (Mei's voice)

1. **Title screen.** Lovely ink-wash art. But the corner says 武俠戰棋 and the logo says 劍影江湖. Which is the name? A small thing, but it threw me a little. *Curious.*
2. **Opening film.** Pretty, and there was a 略過 button right away, so I didn't feel trapped. I skipped after a few seconds because my break is short. *Relieved.*
3. **序幕 story pages.** The big dialogue line was easy to read, but the narration line above it is tiny grey text. There is a 上一句 button from page 2, which I liked. Then I hit 「白子勝滿十局，放還本尊。中道而敗，收子入盤」 and had to read it twice. Later 韋小寶 explained it plainly ("win ten, you go home; lose once, into the box"). I loved that, and wished the game always talked like him. *Charmed, then a bit slow.*
4. **抓子 (choose heroes).** My 小龍女 and 黃蓉 were right there, with portraits and their novels. I could tell who was who. But I could **not** tell what each one is good at. Each card shows one art name with a little icon and nothing else. I long-pressed a card hoping for details and nothing happened. I tapped 局規 hoping for rules and got more story. I took 小龍女 because I love her, not because I understood anything. *Happy about the cast, lost about the choice.*
5. **Making her leader.** The 立為領隊 button is tiny, and the one under 黃蓉 sat under the bottom bar. The text says if the leader falls the round ends. Scary, but at least it was said plainly. *Anxious.*
6. **軍情 briefing.** "Defeat 余滄海 before round 12; you lose if 小龍女 falls" is clear and kind. Then the side goal says "end a hero's action on the lamp at row 9, column 9." The little map has no row or column numbers, and the legend 支線燈記 is 12 px. Where is row 9? *Feeling stupid.*
7. **整備, 陣容 tab.** The goals box sits on top again, so my heroes start below the fold. I tapped 小龍女's card expecting her details and it only got a highlight. The numbers 氣血 / 內力 / 攻擊 / 防禦 / 移動 / 射程 are small but understandable. 可參悟 3 took me a moment: does it mean 3 points to spend? *Okay.*
8. **武學 tab.** So many stacked bars: title, tabs, hero chips, hero name, sub-tabs, a book title, a paragraph of poetic prose. The actual tree only shows after scrolling. When I tapped 玉蜂針 the right pane said 未扣防威力 24, 穿透 60% 防禦, 中毒 · 每回合失去 6 氣血 · 3 回合, 回身. Poison I understood. "Power before defence" I guessed. 回身 was explained only when I opened the folded 招式解說, and then it was explained well. I spent the point with about 70 % confidence. *Proud but unsure.*
9. **Learning does not equip.** I learned 玉蜂針 and nearly went to battle without it. A 前往招式配置 link saved me, and slot-picking worked. Without that link I'd have been confused in battle. *Saved by a link.*
10. **First battle board.** Beautiful. 小龍女 was already selected, so I tapped her to look, and that **used her move**: she "stayed in place" and the action menu opened. I pressed 撤步 to undo, then tapped 黃蓉. Instead of switching to her, the game tried to move 小龍女 to 黃蓉's square. Later 黃蓉's "stay" could not be undone at all. *Confused and a bit annoyed.*
11. **Attacking.** I tried to hit an enemy that looked right next to 楊過. It opened the enemy's info card instead, with no message that he was out of reach. It took three tries and the grid overlay before I understood that diagonal doesn't count on this tilted board. *Felt stupid.*
12. **交鋒預覽 (attack preview).** This is great! Faces, HP bar going 47 → 25, "−22", "敵方還擊 無", poison spelled out in words. It is exactly what I wanted everywhere. *Delighted.*
13. **Little marks on enemies.** Tiny tags 毒, 閉, 穴 and then a red 凶 appeared. I could guess 毒 and 閉穴, but 凶 was never explained anywhere I could find. *Uneasy.*
14. **藥囊.** Opened for 楊過, who had full HP, because he was the selected hero. The medicine box also has 兵器 / 護具 filters, which made no sense there. Once I picked the right hero, using 金創藥 was easy, and "保留行動" meant she could still act. *Fiddly but fine.*
15. **Reload mid-battle.** The main screen offered 繼續第一局, and I was back in round 3 with the same HP and acted states. *Trust earned.*
16. **After the win.** The 韋小寶 jokes made me laugh (玉皇大帝是我姐夫). The bit where 程靈素 appears mid-gesture, about to suck poison from a hand that isn't there, gave me goosebumps. *Moved.*
17. **三盅通吃 cup game.** It shows three prizes, shuffles three cups, I pick one, it's free. Cute, and it's plainly said that the prize goes to 隊伍公庫. I wasn't sure at first if it cost money, but "免費揭一盅" was there in small print. *Fun.*
18. **Shop shelves.** Prices with a coin icon, 庫存 / 持有, and an expand arrow per item. Clear enough. Expanded texts like 「普攻【走勁】餘勁躍向主目標兩格內最近的另一敵人，威力 32%。等距時取靠北、再靠西者。」 are a wall of jargon for me. It is also not said **who** can use a weapon. *Half clear.*
19. **整理隊伍 from the shop.** Dragging 黃蓉 to the first slot worked. 任為領隊 was hidden far down under the hero pane. I gave her 《綿掌》, but 修習 stayed grey with no reason given until I noticed 「修習條件 固本一重」 and went to learn 固本 first. *Puzzle-solved, a bit tired.*
20. **Board 2 camera.** The 視角 panel is clear (左轉 / 俯視 / 右轉 / zoom / 定位俠客 / 地形圖). Top-down view made adjacency much easier! I wish it had been the default, or suggested in the guide. *Relieved.*
21. **Healing with 程靈素.** Choosing 藥王方脈 showed no banner telling me who I could heal, and no tiles lit on allies. I tapped 小龍女 and nothing happened. Later 任盈盈's self-heal showed a lovely 運功療傷 preview ("+30, 解去中毒"). *Mixed.*
22. **Guide (指南).** The help texts are good when you find them (走位與地形 explains water, bridges, 撤步; 出手節奏 explains 連環, 回身, 號令, 調息). But it lives in 選單 → 指南, and nothing on the battle screen points there. *"Why didn't you tell me earlier?"*
23. **Log (戰場紀錄).** It has a 劇情 filter, so I can reread story lines. But the entries are out of order (回合 02, then 01, then 04…), and earlier boards' lines are mixed in. *Nice idea, muddled.*
24. **Losing.** The defeat talk was gentle. 韋小寶 says 「買下的東西放我這兒，丟不了。下回早些來。」, which is kind and clear. The 眼破 page shows "此輪破局 2/10", that purchases carry over and who travelled with me. But "累積的悟 2" means nothing to me, and the main button is the poetic 再入夢 instead of "start again". *Sad but reassured.*
25. **Guarding the qin.** 軍情 said plainly: protect 七弦琴 until round 8 ends, or beat 左冷禪. The top-left badge counts down 守 · 尚須 8 → 7 → 6 → 5 回合, which I loved. Tapping the qin showed its HP. On the board, though, its little HP bar hides under the heroes' bars, so I couldn't see at a glance if it was being hurt. *Clear rule, cramped board.*

## 3. Findings

### L2-01 Hero selection gives no sense of role or strength
- **Severity:** Major · **Type:** Understanding
- **Where:** 抓子 (first hero pick)
- **What happened:** Cards show portrait, name, novel and one art name with an icon. They show no role ("healer", "tank", "ranged"), no stats, and no long-press or info button. Long-pressing a card did nothing. The 局規 fold holds story text, not rules. **Expected:** one plain line per hero ("近身劍客，傷害高" / "遠程暗器，能下毒") or a tap-for-details.
- **Steps:** New game → skip film → 看棋盤 → look at the five cards → long-press one → open 局規.
- **Evidence:** l2/006-afterstory.png, l2/008-pick-long.png, l2/012-rules.png
- **Confidence:** Reproduced
- **Suggestion:** Add a one-line role tag and the stat row already shown in 整備.

### L2-02 Tapping the selected hero on the board spends her move ("stay in place")
- **Severity:** Major · **Type:** Flow / Feedback
- **Where:** Battle, start of every player phase
- **What happened:** The first hero is auto-selected. Tapping her own tile to "look at her" commits a zero-step move and opens the action menu. For 黃蓉 that "stay" then showed only 招式配置, with no 撤步, so it could not be undone. **Expected:** tapping a selected unit shows info, or offers undo for the stay as well.
- **Steps:** Board 1 round 1 → tap 黃蓉 (auto-selected) on her own tile → open the action menu → no 撤步.
- **Evidence:** l2/045-r1c.png, l2/047-r1e.png, els output (no 撤步 entry)
- **Confidence:** Reproduced (also on 楊過 and 任盈盈)

### L2-03 After 撤步 the hero stays selected, so tapping another hero moves the first one instead
- **Severity:** Minor · **Type:** Flow / Touch
- **Where:** Battle
- **What happened:** After undoing 小龍女's move, I tapped 黃蓉 to switch. The game treated it as a move order for 小龍女 (toward 黃蓉's square). **Expected:** tapping a friendly unit switches selection.
- **Steps:** Select hero A → move → 撤步 → tap hero B.
- **Evidence:** l2/043-undo1.png → l2/044-r1b.png
- **Confidence:** Seen once

### L2-04 Tapping an enemy out of reach opens its info card with no "out of range" message
- **Severity:** Major · **Type:** Feedback / Understanding
- **Where:** Battle, choosing a target
- **What happened:** With an art armed, tapping an enemy that looks adjacent on the tilted view (but is diagonal or 2 tiles away) opens 敵情查探 silently. It happened 4 times across 3 boards. **Expected:** a toast such as 「不在射程內（只算縱橫格）」 and the in-range tiles flashing.
- **Steps:** Board 1 round 1 → move 楊過 beside the scout → pick 蛤蟆功 → tap the scout. Same on 空山琴臺 with 令狐冲 → 費彬 and 石壁劍招 → 嵩山弟子.
- **Evidence:** l2/052-r1j.png, l2/059-r1p.png, guard board text logs
- **Confidence:** Reproduced

### L2-05 Adjacency is very hard to judge in the default tilted camera on a short screen
- **Severity:** Major · **Type:** The board / Understanding
- **Where:** All boards
- **What happened:** At 390 px tall the default oblique view makes diagonal tiles look adjacent, and pieces overlap their neighbours' HP bars. 俯視 (top-down) and the grid overlay fix most of it, but the guide never suggests them and they are off by default. **Expected:** top-down plus grid by default on short screens, or a hint the first time a target is out of range.
- **Evidence:** l2/060-r1q.png vs l2/118-b2r1.png (top-down)
- **Confidence:** Reproduced

- **Where:** 整備, battle, enemy info, shop
- **What happened:** These terms had no explanation on screen or one tap away. Some are explained in 選單 → 指南, 3 to 4 taps away and never pointed to:
  - **凶** (red tag on an enemy): explained nowhere I found

  - **根基 · 根基** (the label is duplicated)
  - **可修** vs **可參悟** vs **已習** vs **未習** (four status words for two ideas)
  - **參悟**
  - **授業武譜** vs **門派武學**
  - **歷練 88／100**
  - **累積的悟**
  - **支線燈記**
  - **起陣格**

  - **普攻【定勢】**, **【走勁】**, **【留隙】**, **護持招式【餘氣護身】** (shop items)

  - **眼** (leader = 「這一輪的眼」)
  - **境界** (利劍 / 軟劍 / 重劍 / 木劍 / 無劍, shown on 抓子 as 「利劍境界　黑子如常」)

  **Explained well:** 回身 (in 招式解說 and on the post-attack prompt), 中毒 and 閉穴 (written out in the preview), 坐鎮 (enemy info), terrain such as 渡口茶箱 (toast).

- **Suggestion:** Make every term tappable for a one-line gloss, and add a 「名詞」 page to 指南.

## 4. Verdict

| | Score /10 |
|---|---|
| First impression | 8 |
| Clarity | 4 |
| Comfort on this format (phone landscape) | 5 |
| Desire to keep playing | 6 |

**Three best things**
1. The cast and the writing. Seeing 小龍女 and 黃蓉 together, 程靈素 appearing mid-gesture, and 韋小寶's patter all felt lovingly done.
2. The attack and heal previews tell you exactly what will happen, in words.
3. Progress is safe. Reload anywhere and it's all there, and after a loss the shop keeps your goods.

**Three worst things**
1. Too many unexplained terms (凶, 盟令, 蓄勢, 未扣防威力, 重, 可修 / 可參悟, 悟…). I felt stupid more often than challenged.
2. On the tilted board I couldn't tell who was in reach. Taps on out-of-range enemies just opened an info card.
3. 整備's 武學 and 陣容 tabs on a 390 px screen: the content I needed (the tree, the learn button, the leader button) was always several scrolls away under repeated headers and prose.

**The single change that would help Mei most:** make every rule word and status tag tappable for a one-line plain explanation, and add a short guided first battle that teaches select → move → art → preview (including "only straight lines count").

**Would she come back tomorrow?** Probably yes, for the story and her favourite characters, and because nothing is lost if her break ends mid-battle. But she would play mostly for the talk scenes. She would hope a friend explains the fighting, because the game itself doesn't yet.

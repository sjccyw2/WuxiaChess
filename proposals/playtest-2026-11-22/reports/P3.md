# P3 report: 陳老師 on a phone held upright

## 1. Who I was and what I covered

**Persona:** 陳老師, 58, a retired secondary-school Chinese teacher in Tainan. He has read all of 金庸 many times. He wears reading glasses, taps slowly and is afraid of pressing the wrong thing. He knows Chinese chess but no gaming conventions.
**Format:** phone portrait, 390 × 844, touch only. I also tried 844 × 390, 360 × 640, 320 × 568 and 768 × 1024.

**What I covered:**
- **Title screen.** I read all 16 sections of the guide (遊玩指南) and tried all three settings and the header sound and full-screen buttons. I reloaded to check each setting was kept.
- **Reference material.** I read 群俠列傳, then followed the 江湖誌 link and browsed the wiki for about ten minutes: home, menu, 群俠列傳, 蕭峰's page, 俠客對照, 棋局輿圖 and the 谷底棋臺 map, search, the 降龍廿八掌 art page, and the story page 英雄的入境時刻.
- **New game.** I watched part of the opening film and skipped it. I read the prologue and the 12-line 臨陣 scene, chose heroes (抓子) and looked through 整備 (陣容 and 武學). I played 2 full rounds of 青竹渡 and the start of round 3.
- **Developer mode.** I used 開五重境界 and looked closely at the 選境界 page.
- **Last board, first attempt (跳至終局, 谷底棋臺).** I played honestly. I moved 無名 next to the bosses in round 1 and he died in the enemy turn of round 2, so this was a real loss, not 速敗. I read the losing scene, the 通吃坊 that follows and the 終局未成 summary. I did not press 速敗 afterwards, because the honest loss already showed what losing says and offers.
- **Last board, second attempt.** I played more carefully, healed with medicine and reached round 4. I pressed 局主半血 twice and watched more enemies arrive. I did not play a further round after that; I went straight to 速勝.
- **The ending.** I watched the 4-line closing scene and the finale film (about 59 s) through to its end card, then read the 放下 epilogue. I reloaded and looked at the title, the 選境界 page, the new-run 抓子 page (with the 無名入盤 note) and the 臺邊的五行字 record.
- **Robustness.** I turned the phone, sent it to the background (`hide`) and reloaded at these moments: the title, the opening film, a story page, 抓子, 整備, mid-battle with a hero selected, the shop, and the finale film. I tried the browser's back button on 抓子. I tried 360 × 640, 320 × 568 and 768 × 1024 on the title, 整備 and a battle.

**Not reached:** buying anything in a shop after a normal win, a full honest 10-board run, and the 重看終局 replay itself (I saw the button but did not press it).

**Rig trouble:**
- **Commands reached another tester's browser at first.** For my first few calls, the provided `ctl.mjs` was answered by P2's browser on port 9302. Two of my screenshots landed in P2's folder (`p2/003-guide1.png`, `p2/007-check.png`), and one of my taps opened the guide in P2's browser. I switched to my own small HTTP client (`p3/tools/ctl3.mjs`, which bypasses the proxy) and every call reached port 9303 after that.
- **The 進入遊戲 link opened a second game tab.** This made screenshots time out until I closed the extra tabs.
- **Enemy turns ran 40 to 50 s on the last board.** That is probably the shared machine and I do not count it as a finding.

## 3. Findings

### P3-01 Finale and opening films restart from the beginning when the phone is turned
- **Severity:** Major
- **Type:** Bug
- **Where:** the opening film (序幕) and the finale film
- **What happened:** Turning the phone swaps the upright film for the wide version (`finale-film.mp4` ↔ `finale-film-wide.mp4`) and playback restarts at 0 s. At 53.8 s, one turn sideways put it at 0.82 s, and turning back again put it at 0.86 s.
- **What I expected:** The film continues at the same moment.
- **Steps to reproduce:** Win the last board → 放下 → while the film plays, `viewport 844 390`, then read `video.currentTime`.
- **Evidence:** `eval` output: `[53.825, finale-film.mp4]` → `[0.821, finale-film-wide.mp4]` → `[0.858, finale-film.mp4]`. The landscape frame is plain black with only the two buttons: `p3/132-film-end-land.png`.
- **Confidence:** Reproduced (finale twice; the opening film also showed about 7.5 s after a turn)
- **Suggestion:** Carry `currentTime` across when switching the source.

### P3-02 On the last board 求敗 cannot be told apart from the other bosses
- **Severity:** Major
- **Type:** Understanding
- **Where:** 谷底棋臺 battle
- **What happened:** Every enemy, 求敗 included, carries the same red 主 tag. Once 局主半血 brings more on, 11 figures stand in a clump with overlapping tags. 求敗 differs only by a yellow health bar. The win condition is about him alone.
- **What I expected:** A distinct marker for 求敗 (for example 求 or 敵首), or a button that centres on him.
- **Steps to reproduce:** 跳至終局 → start the battle → look at the board. Then press 局主半血 twice.
- **Evidence:** `p3/099-fb1.png`, `p3/126-f2-half.png`, `p3/128-f2-half2zoom.png`
- **Confidence:** Reproduced
- **Suggestion:** Give him his own tag and show his health on the top bar.

### P3-03 The leader can be wiped out in one enemy turn with no warning
- **Severity:** Major
- **Type:** Feedback
- **Where:** 谷底棋臺
- **What happened:** In round 1 I moved 無名 (292 health) next to the boss group to strike 求敗. In the next enemy turn he fell and the run ended. The only hint is 偏好 專挑領隊, at 11 px, deep in 求敗's scout panel. Nothing in the move preview or the briefing says that moving into that zone exposes the leader to six bosses. On my second try a pull skill (鎖脈擒龍) dragged him into the crowd anyway.
- **What I expected:** A warning when the leader ends a move in many enemies' reach, or the enemy range shown on by default on this board.
- **Steps to reproduce:** 跳至終局 → move 無名 2 to 3 tiles forward toward the centre and attack → end the turn.
- **Evidence:** `p3/104-f-mv.png`, `p3/111-f-r3b.png` (戰役結束 in round 2)
- **Confidence:** Seen once
- **Suggestion:** Put 「求敗與局主專挑領隊」 in the 戰局 briefing text.

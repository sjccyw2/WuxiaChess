# D3 playtest report: 劍影江湖 (desktop 1440 × 900, persona 子軒)

All screenshot paths are under `scratch/d3/`, written below as `d3/…`. Files named `d3/M-*.png` are contact sheets. Each sheet shows one screen at seven window sizes, in this order: 1920×1080, 2560×1080, 1280×720, 1024×768, 900×700, 700×900, 500×900.

## 1. Who I was and what I covered

**Persona.** 子軒, 23, a final-year visual-communication student in Taipei who also works part-time as a UI designer. He likes ink-painting aesthetics and knows 金庸 from the films and a couple of novels. He judges finish, consistency and hierarchy, and he keeps resizing his window.

**What I covered.**
- **Without developer mode:**
  - The site's front page and the title screen.
  - The whole guide (遊玩指南): all 16 sections, opened and read.
  - Settings: every toggle, a reload to check they were kept, and the reduce-motion option (完整動作與特效).
  - The opening film: watched part of it, resized it while it played, then skipped it.
  - Prologue story pages, 抓子 (hero pick), the first board's opening dialogue, 軍情, 整備 on all three tabs.
  - **Three honest rounds** on 第一局 青竹渡, plus the start of round 4.
- **Window sizes.** All seven sizes on the title, a story page, the hero-pick page, 整備 (each of its three tabs), a battle with the arts list open, the shop and the guide. I also tried 720×450, which behaves like a 200% zoom, on the battle, the title and a later battle.
- **Keyboard.** Tab, Shift+Tab, Enter, Space and Escape on the title, guide, settings, story, 整備, shop and 選境界.
- **With developer mode:**
  - I won board 1 with 速勝 to reach the shop and played 三盅通吃.
  - I opened all five 境界 and studied the 境界 page.
  - **Last board, attempt 1.** I played 谷底棋臺 honestly and lost at round 2.
  - **Last board, attempt 2.** I played four honest rounds, pressed 局主半血 twice (more enemies arrived), then finished with 速勝.
  - I watched the ending film to its end, with resizes partway through, then read the pages after it, the title afterwards and the new-run page.
  - The honest loss of attempt 1 covered the defeat path, so I did not also use 速敗.
  - 試局 on 紅楓驛, 桃花石陣, 寒玉石室, 西夏夜苑 and 黑木繡閣. On each I turned the camera, zoomed and played one round.
- **The wiki (江湖誌)** at 1440 and at 700 wide: home, the menu, a hero page, 俠客對照 (adding, removing and re-sorting), a board's map page, an art's page, search, the rules index and a design-document page.

**What I did not do.**
- I did not try 200% zoom on 整備, because by then 試局 had replaced the save. I did try it on the title and on a battle.
- I did not resize the window while a panel's open/close animation was playing. I only resized with panels already open and during the films.
- I checked only briefly what the reduce-motion option changes (see D3-07).

**Rig trouble.**
- One screenshot timed out early on (the browser was busy loading), and the batch resize scripts sometimes ran past the command timeout. None of this needed a browser restart.
- 試局 replaced my save, so the title later read 繼續第九局. That is developer-tool behaviour and is not reported.

## 2. How it felt: a play diary

1. **Front page.** A big brush title, 劍影江湖, then two cards. But the game card is called 一念江湖, and once I'm in the game the header says 武俠戰棋. Three names in two clicks. I frowned: is this the same product?
2. **Title screen.** Gorgeous. A low-poly pavilion in mist, a brush-stroke wordmark, and a calm red primary button with ghost secondaries. Hierarchy is right and type is consistent. Curious and delighted.
3. **The guide.** The button says 遊玩指南, the modal says 操作指南. Inside are 16 accordions of very dense prose with no pictures, and the key terms (連環, 露空, 吸勁…) are not bolded. I opened them all and felt like I was reading a rulebook PDF. Respect for the depth, but I'd never read this as a player. On the good side: the focus ring is crisp and Escape returns focus to the opener. Someone cared.
4. **Settings.** Three switches and a save note: tidy, but thin. Sound is off by default and there is no volume slider. The switches are small and the row labels are not clickable. 章回存檔 is indented differently from the rows above. Everything was kept after a reload. Mild "fine".
5. **Opening film.** Ink-and-manga panels with vertical captions are beautiful, and the cover-crop on ultra-wide works. Then I narrowed the window to sit it beside another app: the film went black and restarted from 0:00. Going back to wide restarted it again. Annoyed: I resize all the time.
6. **Escape on the first story page.** It threw away the whole prologue and dropped me on the title; 入夢 then replayed the film. Lost and a little cross.
7. **Story dialog.** A painterly portrait on parchment beside a navy frame. Lovely, but this is the second art style after the ink film, and the board will be a third (low-poly). Focus starts on 略過 rather than 下一句. Enter and Space do nothing. The speaker name jumps up and down from line to line. Designer twitch.
8. **抓子.** The card grid is pretty. 小龍女's name wraps when no other does, and the round tabs at the card tops look clipped. On ultra-wide the dialog floats small in a sea of background. Polite nod.
9. **軍情 → 整備.** The last story button says 進入整備 but opens 軍情, which has its own 進入整備. Then 整備 repeats 軍情 in a box at the top. Three layers of tabs. Still, the hero cards and the stat panel are clean. Impressed but tired.
10. **First moves on 青竹渡.** Pieces stand on teal and red discs, and that reads well. The arts panel opens right beside the hero and hides the enemy I want to hit. Four times I tapped an enemy that was out of reach and got a full-screen 敵情查探 card instead of a light "too far". Frustrated: the board does not tell me clearly what is adjacent.
11. **Attack preview.** 交鋒預覽 is excellent: current → new HP for both sides, counter-damage and cost. Proud of my first hit.
12. **End of turn.** I pressed E, then Enter: it cancelled, because focus sits on the ×. During the enemy turn 段譽 fell and nothing told me; he was simply gone. My leader stood hidden behind three enemy figures. Anxious.
13. **Shop and 三盅通吃.** Warm cream cards on navy, crisp item art. The cup game with 韋小寶 is a delight. Then I closed the shop with the × (labelled 返回主畫面) and found myself on the title with a button reading 返回通吃坊. Confused.
14. **選境界.** A clean list with brushed seal numbers, and the primary button updates its label (入夢・軟劍境界). But the "selected" style and the "focused" style look alike. Satisfied.
15. **The last board's briefing.** The goal is clear: bring 求敗 down to 1 point of health. The 13×13 diagram is clean. Curious and excited.
16. **The last board, attempt 1.** Seven figures bunched in one corner, each with a red 主 badge on top of the others' health bars. I could not tell who was who without clicking each one. One push, and 無名 went from 292 to 41 in a single enemy turn and fell the next. "……我沒有撐住。" Crushed, and impressed that the defeat has its own text and illustration.
17. **The last board, attempt 2, rounds 1–4.**
    - Defensive play. With the enemy-range overlay on, purple over green turns to mud.
    - Turning the camera 180° and zooming in finally showed the crowd as people.
    - Tapping a boss's body often hit the tile behind him.
    - 郭靖 fell offstage without notice.
    - The two 局主半血 arrivals: a three-second toast the first time, the second time I saw nothing. They just appeared in the clump.
    - Engaged, but squinting.
18. **The ending film.** Stunning. The vertical caption 那根枯枝，求敗沒有再舉起來。 over an ink valley gave me chills. Narrowing the window mid-film made it jump back. The end card 獨孤求敗 with 重看 / 繼續 is elegant, though its flat buttons do not match the game's bevelled ones.
19. **放下 and the reveal.** The revelation 「獨孤求敗」 comes inside a scrolling 600 px text box laid over the dimmed battle board. The best line in the game deserved its own page. Moved, and frustrated as a designer.
20. **The title after winning.** Only the button changed, to 查看本局結局. No new scene, no record of past runs. Mild let-down.
21. **Five boards in 試局.** The art direction is beautiful, board after board. But pale heroes melt into the ice of 寒玉石室, red enemies sit on red range tiles in 黑木繡閣, and trees and roofs cover the board when I rotate. Admiring, with notes.
22. **The wiki.** A new tab, a cream-and-forest palette, the same serif. A fine companion. Then its home page spoils the ending in its first paragraph, and the table of contents shows the producer's design documents (「製作人已定案的事」). Surprised.

## 3. Findings

### D3-01: The wiki's home page gives away the ending, and the wiki exposes internal design documents
- **Severity:** Major. **Type:** Understanding / Text.
- **Where:** 江湖誌 home (`/wiki/`), 單輪劇本 section.
- **What happened:**
  - The first paragraph on the wiki's home page ends 「…他才明白那個奧義是『放下』。那個少年，是年輕時的他自己。」. That is the final twist of the game.
  - The game's own top bar links to this page.
  - 單輪劇本 第0節 is titled 「這份文件定了甚麼」 and talks about 「製作人已定案的事」 and 「協作須知」, which is internal collaboration text.
  - The banner reads 「劍影江湖・一念江湖（暫名）」, a placeholder.
  - The home text says the boy 「陪他下了五場」, while the game says 十局 throughout.
  - The ranking table includes 無名 as a playable hero, which is another spoiler.
- **What I expected:** a reference wiki with spoilers hidden or marked, and no internal design notes.
- **Steps to reproduce:** On the title, click 江湖誌 ↗ and read the home page. Then open 單輪劇本 → 0.
- **Evidence:** d3/207-wiki1.png, d3/218-wiki-story.png.
- **Confidence:** Reproduced.
- **Suggestion:** Put a spoiler fold on ending content, and move the design documents out of the player-facing wiki or label them clearly.

### D3-02: Resizing the window during a film restarts it from the beginning
- **Severity:** Major for this persona, Minor for most players. **Type:** Bug.
- **Where:** Opening film and ending film.
- **What happened:**
  - Crossing the aspect-ratio threshold swaps the video file (`prologue-film-wide.mp4` ↔ `prologue-film.mp4`, `finale-film-wide.mp4` ↔ `finale-film.mp4`).
  - Playback restarts at 0:00, with a black frame meanwhile. Measured: 32.7 s → 0.0 for the opening film; 44.9 s → 0.0 for the ending film.
  - Swapping back starts it from 0 again, paused.
  - The log shows aborted media requests.
- **What I expected:** the film keeps its position, or the source is chosen once per playback.
- **Steps to reproduce:** Start 入夢. After about 15 s, run `viewport 700 900`, then `viewport 1440 900`, and read the video's currentTime.
- **Evidence:** d3/017–020, d3/177-efilm-uw.png, d3/178-efilm-narrow.png; eval output `[["prologue-film.mp4","0.0",true]]`.
- **Confidence:** Reproduced (both films).
- **Suggestion:** Carry `currentTime` across the source swap, or do not swap mid-play.

### D3-03: The last board's crowd cannot be read: badges overlap and nothing names the bosses
- **Severity:** Major. **Type:** Layout / Feedback.
- **Where:** 終局 谷底棋臺.
- **What happened:**
  - Six 局主 and 求敗 stand in a tight block. Every one has a red health bar plus a 主 tag, and these overlap each other and the figures behind.
  - There is no name on the board. I had to click each figure to learn it was 洪安通, 余滄海, 左冷禪, 公孫止 or 丁春秋.
  - 求敗's 1700 health appears only inside the 軍情 popup. He has no distinct badge on the board, even though he is the whole objective.
- **What I expected:** the objective unit is marked distinctly (a different badge or a large health bar in the HUD), names appear on hover, and badges do not pile up.
- **Steps to reproduce:** 跳至終局 → 進入整備 → 出陣 → 開戰, then look at the north cluster.
- **Evidence:** d3/147-fb-hover.png, d3/150-fbr2.png, d3/152-fbr2b.png, d3/165-fin2-half.png.
- **Confidence:** Reproduced.
- **Suggestion:** Pin a 求敗 health bar under the round counter, and show name labels on hover.

**Three best things**
1. The art direction. The ink films (the 那根枯枝 caption, the 獨孤求敗 end card), the painterly portraits and ten distinct dioramas are ambitious and mostly beautiful.
2. 交鋒預覽 and the tooltips: before → after health, counter-damage, cost, and terrain labels like 「紅梅翠竹 · 防禦 ＋5」. Information design at its best.
3. Keyboard and focus basics are real: focus is trapped in modals, the ring is visible, Escape closes, focus returns to the opener. The wiki's 俠客對照 radar and table are a pleasure.

**Three worst things**
1. Readability on crowded or busy boards: overlapping 主 badges, no name labels, the objective unit not distinct, overlays that mix to mud, scenery covering pieces.
2. Resilience to window changes: the films restart on resize, fixed-size dialogs float on big screens, and the layout jumps to the phone version on narrow windows.
3. Naming and flow seams: three product names, buttons named differently from what they open, Escape that undoes moves, and the spoiler on the wiki's front page.

**The single change that would most improve it for 子軒:** a legibility pass on the board — names on hover, a pinned health bar for the objective unit, outline-only range overlays, and fade-out for occluding scenery. The game's beauty is already there; this would let him actually read it.

**Would he come back tomorrow?** Yes, for the art and that ending. He would come back with a sketchbook of redlines, and with the window kept at a single size until the films stop restarting.

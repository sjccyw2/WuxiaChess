# Poison: higher damage, stacking, no cap

Proposal, 2026-11-28, asked by the producer after 毒發身亡 was dropped (Q-N176): "I want poison damage to be higher and stackable and no cap per enemy. No other power beyond that." **Nothing in the game was changed.** The questions it raises are Q-N177 to Q-N179 in `ROADMAP.md`; the work is roadmap item 0.71.

So this proposal adds exactly three things: poison deals more, poisons add up, and there is no limit to how many. It adds no spreading, no lowered 防禦, no bonus for felling a poisoned foe and no new art.

## 1. Poison today

- **One poison per piece.** A second poison keeps the stronger damage and the later end (`applyCondition` in `rules/battle-mastery.js`). Poisoning a foe twice adds nothing.
- **Flat, small ticks.** It bites once at the end of each enemy turn. What the heroes lay is 5 to 12 a turn: 黑血神針 7 for 2 turns, 玉蜂針 6×3, 赤蠍粉 7×3, 七心海棠 12×3 (once a board), the manuals 冰魄銀針 5×2 and 千蛛萬毒手 8×3, and 程靈素's leader boon 碧蠶毒蠱, 4×2 on every hero's blow.
- **Enemies grow; poison does not.** In 重劍境界 a troop has about 60 氣血 on board 1, 150 on board 5 and 400 on board 10; a master 116, 299 and 700–970. A 7-a-turn poison is a tenth of a troop on board 1 and 2% of one on board 10.
- It passes 金剛, cannot take a 不敗 piece below 1, is credited to whoever laid it, and is cleared by 九花玉露丸, 天山雪蓮, 易筋經 and the antidote forms. 引爆餘毒 (`distill`) spends what is left at once, up to 16.

## 2. The three rules

1. **Stacking.** Each poison is a layer of its own, with its own damage and its own last round. At the end of each enemy turn the layers' damage is added together and bites once. A layer ends on its own; the others go on.
2. **No cap.** There is no limit to the layers on a piece.
3. **Higher damage**, on poison a hero lays. Three ways to raise it (Q-N177):

| Way | Rule | 赤蠍粉 per layer, board 1 / 5 / 10 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **A. Grows with the hero (recommended)** | damage × (1 + the hero's level ÷ 5), rounded | 8 / 17 / 21 |
| B. Doubled | damage × 2 | 14 / 14 / 14 |
| C. Doubled, plus the hero's level | damage × 2 + level | 15 / 21 / 24 |

A leaves the first boards almost as they are (a level-1 hero's poison is ×1.2) and triples poison by level 10, so it keeps pace with the foes, as the heroes' own blows do. B is simplest, but it is strongest where poison is already strong and fades again late. C is strongest everywhere.

Rule words, as the guide would say them (`data/rule-terms.js`): 「中毒：每次中毒各成一層，各自計回合；每個敵方回合結束時，各層毒傷相加，扣一次氣血。層數沒有上限。」 and, for A, 「俠客所下的毒，毒傷隨施毒者等級增長：每 5 級多一倍底數。」

## 3. What a dedicated poisoner does

A hero using the same poison art on the same foe every turn reaches a steady number of layers equal to the art's turns. Damage per turn at that steady state:

| Art | Today | Stacking only | A | B | C (level 10) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 赤蠍粉 7×3, board 1 / 5 / 10 | 7 / 7 / 7 | 21 | 24 / 51 / 63 | 42 | 72 |
| 黑血神針 7×2 | 7 | 14 | 16 / 34 / 42 | 28 | 48 |
| 玉蜂針 6×3 | 6 | 18 | 21 / 42 / 54 | 36 | 66 |
| 千蛛萬毒手 8×3 | 8 | 24 | 30 / 57 / 72 | 48 | 78 |
| 碧蠶毒蠱, four heroes on one foe | 4 | 32 | 40 / 80 / 96 | 64 | 144 |

Under A, a dedicated poisoner on board 10 takes about a sixth of a troop each turn on her own, and a 碧蠶毒蠱 team focusing one foe about a quarter, on top of their blows. That is the poison team the producer described: 程靈素 leading, her boon putting a layer on every blow, with 任盈盈, 小龍女 or a 千蛛萬毒手 learner adding theirs. No new power is needed for it.

## 5. What stays as it is

- No spreading, no lowered 防禦, no bonus for felling a poisoned foe, no new art (the producer's limit).
- The cures clear every layer at once. 莽牯朱蛤 keeps every layer off. 金剛 lets poison through; 不敗 still holds at 1.
- Each tick is credited to whoever laid the newest layer, for kills, the record and the 支線.
- Enemy numbers are unchanged; under A, B or C only poison a hero lays is raised (Q-N178 asks whether enemy poison stacks too).

## 6. Questions for the producer

- **Q-N177. How much higher.** A (grows with the hero's level, recommended), B (doubled) or C (doubled plus level).
- **Q-N178. Do enemies' poisons stack on heroes too?** Six foes poison: 白駝山蛇奴 (蛇毒) and 神龍教青龍門眾 (步裡藏毒) with their plain blows, and 木高峰 (駝峰毒霧), 摘星子 (星宿毒焰), 李莫愁 (冰魄銀針) and 鮮于通 (金蠶蠱毒) with their signatures. Recommendation: yes, one rule for everyone, with their numbers unchanged; 莽牯朱蛤 and the cures answer it. The simulations below already let enemy poison stack.
- **Q-N179. 引爆餘毒 with layers.** Today it spends the whole poison for at most 16. With layers it would wipe out a teammate's stack for 16. Recommendation: it spends layers, newest first, up to its 16, and leaves the rest. The alternative is to leave it as it is.

## 7. Building it

- **Data.** `conditions.poison` keeps `power` (the layers' sum, which every page and rule already reads) and `through` (the latest end), and gains `layers: [{ power, through, source }]`. A save from before holds one poison, read as one layer.
- **Ticks** prune the layers that have ended, add the rest, and bite once (`nextRound` in `rules/game.js`).
- **The board and the pages** show 中毒 ×3 · 每回合 63; the strike forecast says 中毒 ＋21（共 3 層）.
- **The simulated player** values poison only through a blow's immediate damage, so it seldom stacks one foe (§4). It should be taught a layer's worth (its damage times its turns), or the balance tool will keep understating poison.
- Checks: stacking, expiry layer by layer, cures, 莽牯朱蛤, crediting, old saves; `check-skills` for the six poison arts; `sim-battles` at two seeds.

# WuxiaChess Roadmap

> Started 2026-11-20. Owner: the producer (repository owner). Maintained by every agent that works here.
> The design documents stay the source of truth for story and rules: `wiki/content/世界觀設定.md` (world view, v1.0) and `wiki/content/單輪劇本.md` (single-run script, v1.0), both written into the game. This file tracks **what to build next, in what order, and what is waiting on the producer**.

## How to use this file

- **Agents: read this file before starting work**, and update it in the same commit as your work: tick items, add items you discover, and record decisions.
- **Questions for the producer go in [Open questions](#open-questions).** Ask in the session first. If the producer has not answered by the time you finish, add the question there (next free ID, your workstream, the options and your recommendation) instead of leaving it only in chat, a PR body or a design document. Build on your recommended default only where the work can be undone easily, and say so in the entry.
- When the producer answers, move the entry to [Decisions](#decisions), and carry the answer into the design document it affects.
- Do not remove a question because you think it is resolved. Only the producer resolves questions.

## Where the game stands

- **Playable:** the prologue (on a first visit a 45-second film, then the youth finds he cannot recall his name and calls himself 無名; later runs a short prologue in pages), a run of ten boards drawn from fifteen (since 2026-11-22; five of them each built round one villain: 神龍竹堂, 絕情花徑, 鐵掌溪灘, 西夏夜苑, 黑木繡閣), twenty heroes (令狐冲, 任盈盈, 向問天, 郭靖, 黃蓉, 小龍女, 楊過, 段譽, 張無忌, 胡斐, and since 2026-11-22 蕭峰, 虛竹, 洪七公, 黃藥師, 張三丰, 趙敏, 程靈素, 狄雲, 袁承志, 阿青; a run draws three and can add nine more), thirty named adversaries (a master and a lieutenant on each of the first ten boards, five villains who joined those boards on 2026-11-21, and five who are each the lone 局主 of a board of their own since 2026-11-22), 韋小寶's shop, dice cups and side bets, 求敗 at the table after every board, night talk, the five lines carved by the table, and the five 境界. The old five-volume arc is gone. Since 2026-11-22 the last board and the ending are real: a run in 無劍境界 goes on past its tenth board to 谷底棋臺, where 無名 and 求敗 are both on the board with the ten masters that run met, and winning it plays the ending film (workstream 2).

- **Checks on 2026-11-22** (after the five boards built round one villain): `check-wiki`, `check-story`, `check-mechanics`, `check-skills` (0 failures, 5 warnings), `extra-fonts.py --check` (fonts rebuilt) and `sound-check` all pass.

- **The five 境界 are difficulty levels** (1.2): picked before each run, the next opened by winning the highest one, and in the fifth a run goes on to the last board and the ending (workstream 2). **Three boards carry objects** (0.11 stage two): 守 the 七弦琴 on 空山琴臺, 守 the war drum on 襄陽殘垣, 破 the two 星索 posts on 無刻漏臺. **Developer mode:** `game/?dev` adds a tab to win or lose a battle at once, open all five 境界, jump to the last board, play the ending film or open 無名入盤 (README, "Developer mode").
- **Story is three layers that never refer to each other** (spine, boards, heroes; see README). Any new text must keep that rule, and `node tools/check-story.mjs` must pass.
- **Same-book lines:** a hero from the same series as a board's master gets one line on that board (46 lines, `ENCOUNTER_SCRIPT[].heroLines`), and one against a named black stone of their series set down beside him (11 lines, `ENCOUNTER_SCRIPT[].extras[].heroes`: before the battle if he stands there, as he steps on if he arrives later). Both are matched by `sameSeries` in `game/src/data/hero-voices.js`. Hero-to-hero lines do not exist yet.
- **Heroes start with an early art and climb to the famous one.** Each of the twenty has a class that the player is not shown, a starting art in the 根基 row, and a tree that ends in the art they are known for, which carries a price. Thirty-odd effects were added for this (see workstream 0). `node tools/check-skills.mjs` holds the trees to those rules and `node tools/check-mechanics.mjs` holds the rules engine to its specification.
- **The battle screen has no command bar.** Selecting a hero shows where they can go; the arts list opens beside the hero after a move; End turn stands alone. The whole presentation layer can be switched off piece by piece in `game/src/polish.js` (`game/?classic` shows the original).
- **Boards differ in kind.** **Fifteen boards, ten to a run** (3.4, README "The fifteen boards"): 青竹渡 first, 無刻漏臺 last, and each stage between drawn from a tier of like strength that now also holds a board built round one villain, with no lieutenant and his own troop type in the other three seats. Each board has a primary objective (斬將 or 殲滅), its own number of enemies, reinforcements on seven boards, two masters on 倒影天街, and a secondary drawn each run from the board's pool of twelve kinds. A win alone brings the next hero. The table is `game/src/data/battles.js`. Before 整備 a 軍情 page states all of it, and before round one the player can swap heroes' starting tiles on the board.
- **The art library** (`asset-gen/README.md`): of 30 painted characters, all 10 villains and 10 troop types are in the game (five pairs on the first ten boards since 2026-11-21, five as boards of their own since 2026-11-22), and since 2026-11-22 the 10 heroes as well. The weak rows of the first ten villain and troop sheets are repainted (Q-N34); the ten added on 2026-11-22 are as the pack came (Q-N85). All 8 painted effects and every item picture are in the game (金蛇劍法, 金蛇劍 and 烏蠶衣 came in with 袁承志 and 狄雲). The story pictures that fit the v1.0 story are in the game (1.10), and so is 求敗's portrait (Q-N67). The youth's and 求敗's sprite sheets are in the game since 2026-11-22 (2.3), with the youth's full-length figure, his sword 利劍 and the ending film (`asset-gen/finale-video/`).
- **The story is the new arc (world view v1.0, run script v1.0), written into the game on 2026-11-21 in 金庸's manner.** 獨孤求敗, an immortal who still cannot put down one question (the final secret of martial arts), copied the art of every great fighter into a go board and has played himself for centuries. The player, his own younger self, falls into the board in his sleep. 求敗 sits across the table from the first scene and speaks after every board; heroes and villains are copies (一念) that leave only by winning; he presses 戰意 on his black stones and the youth trusts his white ones. Each run the youth wins is one 境界 (利劍, 軟劍, 重劍, 木劍, 無劍), and 求敗's lines change with it until he sees that the secret is 放下. 韋小寶 walks in and out because the wall stops only those who seek something from martial arts; the secondary is his side bet. The last board and the ending follow the fifth 境界 (workstream 2). **Not built:** a rule change for each 境界 (the five differ only in the enemies' numbers today). See 1.8.
- **The wiki covers the rules.** 戰鬥規則 has a page for every rule word, listing the arts, items and enemies that carry it; the words live in `game/src/data/rule-terms.js`, which the in-game guide reads too. `node tools/check-wiki.mjs` fails when the game gains a mechanic, enemy, board piece or kind of secondary that no page covers.

## 0. Skill trees, enemy behaviour and the battle screen

Goal: no hero starts with their strongest art; every art has a job; heroes and adversaries each play differently; the battle reads and feels like a console tactics game.

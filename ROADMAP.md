# WuxiaChess Roadmap

> Started 2026-11-20. Owner: the producer (repository owner). Maintained by every agent that works here.
> The design documents stay the source of truth for story and rules: `wiki/content/世界觀設定.md` (world view, v1.0) and `wiki/content/單輪劇本.md` (single-run script, v1.0), both written into the game. This file tracks **what to build next, in what order, and what is waiting on the producer**.

## How to use this file

- **Agents: read this file before starting work**, and update it in the same commit as your work: tick items, add items you discover, and record decisions.
- **Questions for the producer go in [Open questions](#open-questions).** Ask in the session first. If the producer has not answered by the time you finish, add the question there (next free ID, your workstream, the options and your recommendation) instead of leaving it only in chat, a PR body or a design document. Build on your recommended default only where the work can be undone easily, and say so in the entry.
- When the producer answers, move the entry to [Decisions](#decisions), and carry the answer into the design document it affects.
- Do not remove a question because you think it is resolved. Only the producer resolves questions.

## Where the game stands

- **Playable:** the prologue (on a first visit a 45-second film, then the youth types his name; later runs a short prologue in pages), ten boards in shuffled order, ten heroes (令狐冲, 任盈盈, 向問天, 郭靖, 黃蓉, 小龍女, 楊過, 段譽, 張無忌, 胡斐), twenty-five named adversaries (a master and a lieutenant per board, plus five villains who joined the boards on 2026-11-21 and now have their own lines and bios), 韋小寶's shop, dice cups and side bets, 求敗 at the table after every board, night talk, the five lines carved by the table, and the five 境界. The old five-volume arc is gone. **Placeholders only:** the last battle and the 放下 ending.
- **Checks on 2026-11-21:** `check-wiki`, `check-story`, `check-skills` (0 failures, the six known warnings), `check-mechanics` and `extra-fonts.py --check` all pass.
- **The five 境界 are difficulty levels** (1.2): picked before each run, the next opened by winning the highest one, and after all five a placeholder last battle and 放下 ending (2.5). **Three boards carry objects** (0.11 stage two): 守 the 七弦琴 on 空山琴臺, 守 the war drum on 襄陽殘垣, 破 the two 星索 posts on 無刻漏臺. **Developer mode:** `game/?dev` adds a tab to win or lose a battle at once, open all five 境界 or jump to the last battle (README, "Developer mode").
- **Story is three layers that never refer to each other** (spine, boards, heroes; see README). Any new text must keep that rule, and `node tools/check-story.mjs` must pass.
- **Same-book lines:** a hero from the same series as a board's master gets one line on that board (23 lines, `ENCOUNTER_SCRIPT[].heroLines`), and one against a named black stone of their series set down beside him (5 lines, `ENCOUNTER_SCRIPT[].extras[].heroes`: before the battle if he stands there, as he steps on if he arrives later). Both are matched by `sameSeries` in `game/src/data/hero-voices.js`. Hero-to-hero lines do not exist yet.
- **Heroes start with an early art and climb to the famous one.** Each of the ten has a class that the player is not shown, a starting art in the 根基 row, and a tree that ends in the art they are known for, which carries a price. Thirty-odd effects were added for this (see workstream 0). `node tools/check-skills.mjs` holds the trees to those rules and `node tools/check-mechanics.mjs` holds the rules engine to its specification.
- **The battle screen has no command bar.** Selecting a hero shows where they can go; the arts list opens beside the hero after a move; End turn stands alone. The whole presentation layer can be switched off piece by piece in `game/src/polish.js` (`game/?classic` shows the original).
- **Boards differ in kind.** Each board has a primary objective (斬將 or 殲滅), its own number of enemies, reinforcements on seven boards, two masters on 倒影天街, and a secondary drawn each run from the board's pool of twelve kinds. A win alone brings the next hero. The table is `game/src/data/battles.js`. Before 整備 a 軍情 page states all of it, and before round one the player can swap heroes' starting tiles on the board.
- **The art library** (`asset-gen/README.md`): of 30 painted characters, 5 villains and 5 troop types are in the game; 5 villains and their troops are kept for new stages; 10 new heroes wait for the producer. The weak rows of the ten villain and troop sheets in the game are repainted (Q-N34). The 7 painted effects and every item picture are in the game. The story pictures that fit the v1.0 story are in the game (1.10), and so is 求敗's portrait (Q-N67). The youth's sprite sheet and card, and 求敗's card and sheet, are painted and wait for the last battle (2.3).
- **The story is the new arc (world view v1.0, run script v1.0), written into the game on 2026-11-21 in 金庸's manner.** 獨孤求敗, an immortal who still cannot put down one question (the final secret of martial arts), copied the art of every great fighter into a go board and has played himself for centuries. The player, his own younger self, falls into the board in his sleep. 求敗 sits across the table from the first scene and speaks after every board; heroes and villains are copies (一念) that leave only by winning; he presses 戰意 on his black stones and the youth trusts his white ones. Each run the youth wins is one 境界 (利劍, 軟劍, 重劍, 木劍, 無劍), and 求敗's lines change with it until he sees that the secret is 放下. 韋小寶 walks in and out because the wall stops only those who seek something from martial arts; the secondary is his side bet. **Not built:** the last battle and the ending (the producer will design them), a rule change for each 境界 (the five differ only in text today), and 求敗's portrait (a placeholder is in the game). See 1.8.
- **The wiki covers the rules.** 戰鬥規則 has a page for every rule word, listing the arts, items and enemies that carry it; the words live in `game/src/data/rule-terms.js`, which the in-game guide reads too. `node tools/check-wiki.mjs` fails when the game gains a mechanic, enemy, board piece or kind of secondary that no page covers.

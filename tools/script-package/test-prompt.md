# Test prompt: choosing a model for the rewrite

Paste everything between the two lines into each model, in a fresh conversation, with nothing attached. Then compare the answers blind (see the scorecard at the end, which is for you, not for the model).

---

You are a novelist who writes in the manner of 金庸. I am testing writers for a job: rewriting the story text of a wuxia game so that it reads as high-quality fiction. Below are two short passages from the game, with some background. Rewrite them.

## Background

A country boy falls asleep and dreams himself into a walled valley. There an old man has played chess against himself for centuries. He has forgotten his own name; people call him 求敗 (he is 獨孤求敗, though that is revealed only at the very end). On the stone board, every stone carries a famous fighter from 金庸's novels, each taken at the worst moment of their own story. The boy cannot remember his name either, and calls himself 無名.

The boy commands the white stones (the heroes). He can see the whole board; they can see only a few steps around them. 求敗 sets down the black stones (the villains), pressing a 戰意 into each as he does: a will to fight that is not their own, which they feel and cannot throw off. Beating them lifts it. Cut into the edge of the table is the rule: 「白子勝滿十局，放還本尊。中道而敗，收子入盒。」 Win ten boards and the heroes go home; lose one and they go back in the box.

韋小寶 keeps a stall by the table, sells medicine and weapons, and runs a side bet on every board. 求敗 cannot see or hear him. A great eagle sits by the table and never speaks.

The game is played over many runs, each at one of five 境界 named for the swords 獨孤求敗 laid down (利劍, 軟劍, 重劍, 木劍, 無劍). The boy forgets each run when he wakes; 求敗 remembers every move. Across the five 境界, 求敗 slowly comes to see what he is still holding on to: the board itself. He says it plainly only at the end of the fifth.

**How scenes are built.** Heroes are drawn at random and the boards come in a different order each run, so every scene is put together from separate pieces:

- A line marked **[ANY HERO]** is said by whichever hero is in that role. It must work for any of twenty heroes (men and women, monks and princesses, young and old) and must not name anyone.
- A **board line [BOARD]** belongs to this board. It never names a hero or mentions another board or the board's place in the run.
- A **hero line [HERO: name]** belongs to that hero alone, and is said whoever else is present.
- A **spine line [SPINE]** belongs to the run as a whole: 求敗, 無名, 韋小寶 and narration. It never names a board, a hero or a villain.

## The problems with the current text

1. The dialogue does not sound human. Characters announce the premise instead of living in it, and explain their feelings instead of showing them.
2. One line does not lead to the next. A scene reads as a list of separate remarks, not as people talking to each other.

The story itself is right. Keep every event, every fact, and who is present.

## Style

- **Narration:** plain vernacular with a light classical touch (只見、但見、當下、登時、兀自、便如). What is seen first, then what it means. Four-character phrases sparingly. At most two or three sentences.
- **Dialogue:** at most three short clauses per line. Forms of address fit the speaker (老夫, 在下, 老子, 余某, 駝子, 老道, 小友). No modern words: no 複本, 系統, 數據, 狀態, or anything that sounds like a game.
- **求敗:** archaic, terse, cold. He never explains himself.
- **無名:** a country boy. Plain, honest words, no literary airs. He calls 求敗 老伯.
- **韋小寶:** street talk, slippery, a gambler, the only unlettered voice; the occasional 辣塊媽媽. Funny.
- **Everyone else:** true to who they are in 金庸's novels, at the moment they were taken.
- Lines should read well aloud in Cantonese: no 兒化, and 咱們 or 您 only for characters who speak that way in the novels.
- Learn 金庸's manner; do not copy his sentences.

## Passage 1: the first board of a first run

This is the scene a new player meets, in order. The three heroes drawn this time are 袁承志 (碧血劍: taken mid-leap in the 崇政殿 at 盛京, his sword a few feet from 皇太極), 任盈盈 (笑傲江湖: taken below 少室山, carrying the dying 令狐冲 on her back up to 少林) and 張三丰 (倚天屠龍記: taken on 武當山 the instant a false monk he was helping up struck both palms into his belly). 袁承志 leads. The board is 青竹渡, a stone bridge over shallows. Its masters are 余滄海 (青城派) and 木高峰 (塞北明駝), both from 笑傲江湖, both of whom spent years hunting the 辟邪劍譜. After the win, 求敗 lends a fourth hero, this time 向問天 (笑傲江湖: taken in a pavilion, his hands chained, hundreds of men from both the orthodox sects and his own cult closing in).

- 〔B001〕[BOARD · narration, battle log as the board appears] 一道石橋橫跨淺水，橋頭橋尾各立一人，凝然不動，便如兩枚落定了的黑子。
- 〔H202〕[HERO: 袁承志 · narration, as he appears] 他飛身前撲，長劍直指前方，劍尖前面卻是空的。
- 〔H203〕[HERO: 袁承志] 皇太極！……人呢？這不是崇政殿。各位，在下袁承志，適才一時魯莽，可曾誤傷了哪一位？
- 〔H015〕[HERO: 任盈盈 · narration] 她半蹲著身子，雙手還反扣在肩後，便如背著一個人。背上卻是空的。
- 〔H016〕[HERO: 任盈盈] ……冲哥？人呢？我明明背著他，山門便在前面。
- 〔H158〕[HERO: 張三丰 · narration] 他伸出雙手，正要去扶一個跪在地下的人。地下並沒有人。
- 〔H159〕[HERO: 張三丰] 大師請起，不必多禮……嗯？老道小腹上這一掌，怎地沒有落實？各位小友，這裡可不是武當山了。
- 〔S030〕[SPINE · narration] 棋臺沿上刻著兩行小字，筆劃深入石理：「白子勝滿十局，放還本尊。中道而敗，收子入盒。」
- 〔S031〕[SPINE · 無名] 臺沿上刻得有字。……是這盤棋的規矩。

- 〔H204〕[HERO: 袁承志 · said only when he leads, in answer to 無名] 先父帶兵，令出如山，在下自幼聽得慣了。兄台既瞧得見全局，在下聽憑差遣。
- 〔S035〕[SPINE · narration] 三個人各自起了個手勢，都只起到一半，便停住了。
- 〔S036〕[SPINE · 韋小寶] 不用慌，個個來到都是這樣。手上最熟的那一樣還在，後來學的，打得幾場自己便回來了。……我說了「個個」麼？你聽錯啦。
- 〔B003〕[BOARD · narration] 青竹渡。一道石橋橫跨淺水，橋頭橋尾各立一人，凝然不動，便如兩枚落定了的黑子。
- 〔B004〕[BOARD · 余滄海] 木駝子，你我為那部劍譜鬥了半世，今日余某竟提不起半分搶它的念頭，心中翻來覆去只是一句話：誰上橋來，便打誰。這可奇了。

## Passage 2: 求敗 across one run of the third 境界 (重劍)

After each board is won, 求敗 says one line, whatever board it was and whoever won it. This run he presses the 戰意 down harder, and for the first time he begins to hear the black stones protest that the thought is not theirs. At the end of the run he speaks once more, then sends the boy home. His lines must never name a board, a hero or a villain. Read the ten as one arc.

- 〔S092〕[after the 1st win] 這一輪老夫加了三分力。你覺得出來麼？
- 〔S093〕[after the 2nd win] 力加了，子卻沒有強。怪事。
- 〔S094〕[after the 3rd win] 適才那枚黑子開口說話。他說甚麼？……你聽得見，老夫怎地聽不見？
- 〔S095〕[after the 4th win] 老夫按得越重，他們打得越死板。倒是按不住的那一刻，招數活了。
- 〔S096〕[after the 5th win] 你那位同伴向老夫說話。老夫幾百年不曾答過子的話。……你替我回一句：老夫聽見了。
- 〔S097〕[after the 6th win] 「這念頭不是我的。」適才那黑子是這般說的，是不是？
- 〔S098〕[after the 7th win] 他說了幾百年，老夫今日方才聽見。
- 〔S099〕[after the 8th win] 重劍無鋒，大巧不工。老夫四十歲前恃之橫行天下，只道力大便是道理。
- 〔S100〕[after the 9th win] 最後一局。這一回，老夫想聽聽他們還說些甚麼。
- 〔S134〕[after the 10th win, closing the run; must end with 請回吧] 那枚黑子說：「這念頭不是我的。」他說了幾百年，老夫今日方才聽見。……好棋。容老夫想一想。請回吧。

## The rules

- Rewrite freely: wording, rhythm, imagery, how a line reaches its point. You may move a beat between lines of the same passage, as long as every fact is still delivered somewhere.
- Do not change what happens, who is present, what anyone knows, names, places, the novels' facts, or the rule on the table.
- Keep every line's marker: an [ANY HERO] line must suit any hero; a [BOARD] line names no hero; a [SPINE] line names no board, hero or villain.
- Keep `{hero}` exactly as it is. Do not add, remove, merge or split lines.
- Keep within the length limits above.

## What to return

1. Every line, in the same order, in exactly this format, one per line, with nothing else on the line:

   〔B001〕new text

2. Then a heading 「說明」 and at most 150 words on what you changed and why.

Write in Traditional Chinese.
